Warm-up · 5 min

Ready–React Dash

Setup: No ball on running days — just legs and reactions. Put a start cone down and a "target" cone about 10 yards straight ahead. You and Dad stand at the start together.

How it works: Dad gives a surprise start signal — a clap, a "GO!", a whistle, or "green!" — and the moment it comes you both explode into a short sprint to the target cone and jog back. Mix the signals up so you have to listen and react, not guess. Sneak in a fake now and then (Dad says "gr… grape!" and nobody moves). Do about 6–8 quick reps with a walk-back between each. Getting warmer and quicker off the mark is the whole point.

Cues: Stay light on the balls of your feet and ready to fire, first two steps short and punchy, and drive the arms hard. Ease off the sprint smoothly — no need to slam the brakes.

🎉 Make it fun: Keep score of who reacts first each rep, and let the winner pick the next start signal (silly voices encouraged). One "gotcha" fake per round — jump early and you owe the other person a dramatic slow-motion replay. 🏁😄

A no-ball reaction-start sprint game — react to a surprise cue and fire out to the cone. Warms the legs, wakes up the nervous system, and drills a fast, explosive first step, all as a race.

Warm-up · 5 min

Lightning Legs Form Drills

Setup: No ball. Use a ~15–20 yard lane with a cone at each end. These are classic running-form drills that teach your legs to move quick, light, and tall — a little tune-up before the real running.

How it works: Do each move down the lane, then walk back easy and start the next one: ankling (tiny quick foot rolls, toes up) → high-knee marches → A-skips → butt-kicks → carioca (grapevine, both directions) → straight-leg bounds → and finish with 2 relaxed build-up strides (start slow, smoothly grow to about 80% by the far cone). About 5 minutes — quality over speed.

Cues: Tall posture, eyes up, quick light contacts with the ground, and toes pulled up toward your shins. Arms drive front-to-back like pistons, not across the body. Springy and smooth, never tense.

🎉 Make it fun: Name each drill after a superhero move — "lightning ankles," "rocket knees" — and Dad does them right beside you, mirror-style. Whoever looks the goofiest doing carioca wins the round. ⚡🦸

A no-ball running-form series — ankling, high knees, A-skips, butt-kicks, carioca, bounds and build-up strides. Grooves quick, light, tall mechanics and primes the legs for the run without any pounding.

Endurance · ~25 min

Treasure Hunt Run-Walk Intervals

Setup: Pick a safe out-and-back route or a loop — sidewalk, path, or a quiet street. No cones needed, though picking a "treasure" landmark ahead (a big tree, a mailbox) to jog toward makes each interval a mini adventure. This is your very first logged run, so we start gentle and build from here.

How it works: Start with a 3-minute easy walk to loosen up, then run this pattern 5 times: jog easy for 2½ minutes → walk 1 minute to recover. That's about 12–13 minutes of actual running today, broken into friendly chunks. Jog toward your next "treasure" landmark each interval. Finish with whatever walk you have left in the block. Log your longest nonstop jog — today it's 2½ minutes, and it'll grow a little every running week.

Cues: Keep the jog at a "talk test" pace — slow enough that you two can chat the whole way. Short, easy strides, relaxed shoulders, breathe in a rhythm. The walk breaks are part of the plan, not a fail — they're what lets the running feel good.

🎉 Make it fun: Turn it into a real treasure hunt — before each jog, one of you calls the next "checkpoint" to reach. Chat and tell stories while you jog (if you can talk, you've got the pace right!). Bank a checkpoint each interval and count your loot at the end. 🗺️🏃

Gentle base-building run-walk intervals — five easy 2½-minute jogs with walk recoveries. The friendly, injury-smart way to grow a 10-year-old's running engine, with a "longest jog" number that climbs each running week.

Speed · ~10 min

Jet Boost Sprints & Chase Races

Setup: Mark a straight lane with cones at 10, 20, and 30 yards. Legs should feel fresh-ish after the run's walk finish. Two parts: build-up "jet boosts," then a timed dash and some chase races.

How it works: Part 1 — Jet Boosts: run three build-up accelerations — one to 10 yd, one to 20 yd, one to 30 yd — starting relaxed and smoothly climbing to near-full speed, then easing off. Walk back between each. Part 2 — Timed dash & chases: run one all-out 20-yard dash and have Dad time it (that's your record to log). Then play handicap chase races — Son gets a big head start and Dad tries to catch him before the finish; adjust the head start so it's close and giggly. 4–5 races.

Cues: On the boosts, build speed gradually — don't rip it from a dead stop. Run tall, drive the knees, punch the arms, and keep the face and hands loose. Full walk-back rest so every sprint is fast and fresh.

🎉 Make it fun: Add rocket "whoosh" sound effects on every jet boost, and let Son set his own head start for the chases — negotiate it like a real deal-maker. Photo-finish celebrations and a made-up medal ceremony for the timed dash. 🚀🏅

Build-up accelerations plus a timed 20-yard dash and playful handicap chase races. Teaches smooth acceleration and top-end speed while keeping it a laughing, close-finish contest — with a dash time to beat.

Cooldown · ~10 min

Walk, Stretch & Chat

Walk it off: Easy 3–4 minute walk together until your breathing settles all the way down. Keep sipping water — even a cloudy morning gets sticky here.

Stretch: Give the running muscles some love — calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, and glutes. Hold each about 20 seconds, relaxed, no bouncing. Roll the ankles out too after all that ankling.

Chat & recap: As you stretch, each name one great thing from today — a long jog you didn't stop, a lightning-fast reaction, a photo-finish chase. Big high-five, then tap a button below so your first run counts. Loved a drill? Tap its ⭐! 🎉

🌧️ Rain Backup (small-space, ~50 min)

Storms roll in or the ground's soaked? No running room needed — a couple of square feet each is plenty. We keep the engine and the speed going with in-place cardio and a bodyweight circuit instead of the road run.

Warm-up (2 rounds of 90 sec): march in place → arm circles & trunk twists → light in-place fast feet. Rest 30 seconds between rounds.

In-place "run" intervals (5 rounds, 2 min on / 1 min easy): alternate steady jog-in-place → high-knee marching → light fast feet for the 2 minutes, then march easy for 1. This stands in for today's run-walk — keep it to a chatty effort.

Speed & power circuit (3 rounds, 30 sec on / 30 sec off), all in place: squats → in-place fast feet → mountain climbers → plank hold → standing broad-jump-and-step-back. Rest a minute between rounds.

Finish: The normal stretch — calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, glutes, ~20 seconds each — then trade one "proud moment" and a high-five.

🎉 Make it fun: Reaction round — Dad claps and you switch instantly (one clap = fast feet, two claps = freeze in a plank). Miss a call and you owe five squats. 😄
