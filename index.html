Warm-up · 5 min

Robbers & Shields Dribble Tag

Setup: Mark a ~10×10-yard box with four cones. You each have a ball inside the box. This is a shielding-and-reaction game — you protect your own ball while hunting the other's, so your head stays up and your feet stay busy.

How it works: Dribble your own ball around the box and try to poke or tap the other person's ball out of the box — while always keeping your own under control. If your ball gets knocked out, do 3 quick toe-taps on it to buy your way back in. Play about 5 minutes, keeping it light and giggly. Count knock-outs if you like, but the real goal is lots of touches and quick looks up.

Cues: Head up to scan, small controlled touches close to your feet, and put your body between the robber and your ball to shield. Change direction sharply the instant someone closes in.

🎉 Make it fun: Whoever pulls off a steal gets to call a silly celebration the other has to copy — robot dance, penguin waddle, your pick. Sneaky fake-outs and spin-aways are totally allowed. 🕵️⚽

A shielding dribble-tag: keep your own ball alive while poking the other's out of the box. Warms up the whole body and drills close control, shielding, and scanning — all with a smile.

Warm-up · 5 min

Countdown Mobility Blastoff

Setup: No ball. Mark a ~15-yard lane with a cone at each end. This is a dynamic mobility flow — each move loosens and warms a different area so the skill work that follows feels smooth and springy.

How it works: Do one move down the lane, then walk back and start the next: leg swings (front-back & side-to-side at the start line) → walking lunges with a twist → inchworms → carioca (grapevine) → skips for height → toy soldiers (straight-leg kicks) → high-knee march. Before the last trip, count down together — "5-4-3-2-1, blastoff!" — and launch into a springy build-up skip. About 5 minutes total.

Cues: Move through a big, controlled range — reach tall, swing loose, no bouncing or forcing. The goal is warm and loose, not tired.

🎉 Make it fun: Each move is a "rocket system check" — call it out mission-control style ("hip thrusters… GO!") and do the countdown together before blastoff. Whoever has the goofiest toy-soldier kicks earns the launch honors. 🚀🧑‍🚀

A no-ball dynamic mobility ladder — swings, lunges, inchworms, carioca, skips, and toy soldiers with a countdown finish. Warms hips, hamstrings, and shoulders so the body is loose and ready to move.

Technical · ~8 min

La Croqueta Gates

Setup: Scatter 4–5 little cone gates (about 2 feet wide) around your area. You'll need your ball. The La Croqueta is Iniesta's and Messi's signature escape — a quick side-shift of the ball from one foot to the other to slip past a defender.

How it works: Dribble up to a gate and, right as you go through, do a La Croqueta: push the ball with the inside of one foot across to the inside of the other foot in one quick two-touch move, shifting it sideways. Then dribble on to the next gate and do it again. Count how many clean La Croquetas you can do through gates in 60 seconds — Dad works the timer.

Cues: One smooth, quick touch to each foot — not a slow roll. Stay low and balanced, shift the ball just enough to change your lane, and sell it like you're beating a real defender. Use both feet.

🎉 Make it fun: Every clean croqueta through a gate is you "nutmegging" an invisible defender — give each one a big commentary shout. Dad tries it too and you vote on who's got the smoother shift. 🪄⚽

Dribble each gate and shift the ball foot-to-foot in one quick La Croqueta. Builds close control, both-footed touch, and a real move to beat defenders with — counted against the clock.

Technical · ~8 min

Rebounder Control-Turn-Pass

Setup: Stand ~4–5 yards from the rebounding wall (any flat wall works). Put a cone about 2 yards to your left and another 2 yards to your right. You'll need your ball.

How it works: Pass firmly into the wall, take the rebound with a controlled first touch, open up and turn toward one of the side cones, then pass back to the wall from that new angle. Next rebound, turn the other way. Keep the receive → turn → pass rhythm going. Count clean control-turn-passes in 60 seconds — a rep counts only if the first touch stays under control.

Cues: Get in line with the ball early, cushion the first touch (soft ankle), and take it across your body into space toward the cone rather than stopping it dead. Open your hips before the ball arrives and keep your head up.

🎉 Make it fun: Dad calls "left!" or "right!" a beat before each rebound so you have to react and turn the way he shouts — miss the call and it's Dad's point. First to 10 clean called turns wins. 🔊🔄

Pass, control the rebound, turn to a side cone, and pass back from the new angle. Builds a clean directional first touch and the receive-turn-play habit that unlocks midfield — reps counted in a minute.

Speed & Agility · ~15 min

Thunderbolt Agility Gauntlet

Setup: Build one line of stations: speed-stick ladder → two trapezoid hurdles → a 4-cone zig-zag weave → a 15-yard sprint finish. No ball for this one — it's pure footwork and speed.

How it works: Run the whole thing as one timed lap: quick feet through the ladder (one foot in each rung), two-footed lateral hops over the trapezoids, sharp cuts weaving through the cones, then explode into the 15-yard sprint through the finish. Walk all the way back and rest, then go again. Log your fastest clean lap — a lap only counts if you hit every rung and cone.

Cues: Quick, light feet in the ladder; land soft and spring right back up over the hurdles; drop your hips and stay low on the cuts; then run tall and pump the arms on the sprint. Full rest between reps — speed needs fresh legs.

🎉 Make it fun: Dad runs the gauntlet too — best clean lap wins, and the loser does a slow-motion victory-lap impression of the winner. Give each lap a superhero name and a "zoom" sound on the sprint. ⚡🏃

A four-station agility gauntlet run against the clock — ladder, hurdles, cone cuts, and a sprint. Trains footwork, change of direction, and acceleration, with a fastest-clean-lap record to chase.

Game · ~15 min

Shooting Star Gallery

Setup: Make a goal with two cones (or use a real goal). Put a target cone in each bottom corner and a "start" cone about 10 yards out with a "defender" cone a couple yards in front of the goal. Dad can play keeper. You'll need your ball and a few spare balls if you have them.

How it works: From the start cone, dribble in, beat the defender cone with a move (a La Croqueta counts!), and shoot. Scoring: hit a corner target = 2 points, score anywhere past the keeper = 1 point, miss = 0. Take 10 shots each and add up your points (best possible is 20). Highest total wins the round. Log your points out of 20.

Cues: Plant your non-kicking foot beside the ball, strike through the middle with your laces, look up to pick your target then down at the ball, and follow through low and firm for accuracy.

🎉 Make it fun: Give every shot a star-player name and do the commentary ("…and it's a worldie!"). Make the 10th shot a double-points finale, and the winner earns keeper-celebration bragging rights. 🌟🥅

A dribble-beat-shoot finishing game with corner-target bonuses and a keeper. Rolls together the day's moves and puts a score on the board — accuracy, composure, and a whole lot of celebrating.

Cooldown · ~5 min

Walk, Stretch & Recap

Walk it off: Easy 2–3 minute walk together until your breathing settles. Keep sipping water — it's heading for a hot one.

Stretch: Give the legs some love — calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, and groin (great for all those cuts and kicks). Hold each about 20 seconds, relaxed, no bouncing.

Recap: As you stretch, each name one great thing from today — a slick croqueta, a corner-target bullseye, a winning gauntlet lap. Big high-five, then tap a button below so it counts. Loved a drill? Tap its ⭐! 🎉

🌧️ Rain Backup (small-space, ~60 min)

Storm sneaks in early or you're stuck inside? No running room needed — a couple of square feet each is plenty. We keep the ball skills going in place and add a bodyweight circuit for the engine.

Warm-up (2 rounds of 90 sec): march in place → arm circles & trunk twists → light in-place fast feet. Rest 30 seconds between rounds.

In-place ball mastery (3 rounds of 40 sec on / 20 off): toe taps on top of the ball → side-to-side sole rolls → foundations (inside-foot taps) → sole pull-push. Tiny space, big touches — quick and controlled.

Strength + speed circuit (3 rounds, 40 sec on / 20 sec off), all in place: squats → push-ups (knees OK) → mountain climbers → plank hold → in-place fast feet. Rest a minute between rounds.

Finish: The normal stretch — calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, groin, ~20 seconds each — then trade one "proud moment" and a high-five.

🎉 Make it fun: Reaction round — Dad claps and you switch instantly (one clap = fast feet on the ball, two claps = freeze in a plank). Miss a call and you owe five squats. 😄
