Warm-up · 5 min

Traffic Signals Reaction Run

Setup: No ball. Mark a lane about 15 yards with a cone at each end. This is a reaction and locomotion game — you move on Dad's signal and switch the instant it changes, so your feet and your ears both wake up.

How it works: Dad is the traffic signal and calls out changes as you travel up and down the lane: "green" = easy jog, "yellow" = high-knee march in place, "red" = freeze, and a surprise "GO-GO-GO!" = a quick 5-second sprint. React as fast as you can on every call. Play about 5 minutes, mixing the calls up so they're never predictable.

Cues: Light, quick feet on the jog, snap to a dead freeze on "red," and explode into the first two steps on "GO." Head up and ears open — the whole game is reacting fast.

🎉 Make it fun: Trade roles so you get to be the signal and try to trick Dad ("greeeee… RED!"). Whoever gets faked out owes the other three funny robot steps. Sneaky fake-outs are totally allowed. 🚦😄

A no-ball reaction warm-up: jog, march, freeze, or sprint on Dad's traffic-signal calls. Fires up the legs, sharpens reaction time, and slips in a few short bursts to prime the body for running.

Warm-up · 5 min

Rocket Legs Form Drills

Setup: No ball. Same ~15-yard lane. This is a running-form flow — each drill teaches a piece of smooth, powerful running so the run that follows feels easier and faster.

How it works: Do one drill down the lane, then walk back and start the next: ankling (tiny quick foot rolls) → A-marches (tall knee-up marches) → A-skips (same, with a spring) → high knees → butt-kicks → straight-leg bounds → 2 build-up strides (start easy, smoothly speed up to about 80%). About 5 minutes total.

Cues: Run tall like a string lifts the top of your head, knees up and toes up, quick light ground contacts, and pump the arms front-to-back (not across your body). Quality over speed — smooth is fast.

🎉 Make it fun: Call each one a rocket "stage" and count down — "3-2-1, ignition!" — then blast off into the build-up stride at the end. Dad drills alongside you and you vote on who had the springiest A-skips. 🚀

A no-ball running-form ladder — ankling, marches, skips, high knees, butt-kicks, bounds, and build-up strides. Teaches tall posture, quick knee lift and smooth arm drive so running feels lighter and faster.

Endurance · ~28 min

Zookeeper Run-Walk Safari

Setup: Pick a flat out-and-back or a loop around the yard/park — no equipment needed, just room to jog. This is the heart of today: your very first base-building run. Because it's run #1, we keep the jogs short and gentle and let the walk breaks do the recovering.

How it works: Imagine you're a zookeeper visiting animals around the safari. Do 4 rounds of: 3 minutes easy jog + 1 minute walk — about 12 minutes of running in all. At each "enclosure" (the end of a jog) give the animal a silly greeting before the walk break. Keep the jog at a talk-easy pace — slow enough to chat the whole time. Log your longest nonstop jog (that's 3 minutes today) as your first endurance record.

Cues: Easy, relaxed shoulders and smooth breathing — if you can't talk, slow down. Land light and quick under your body, not way out in front. Walk breaks are for real recovery, not just a stroll to the next jog.

🎉 Make it fun: Each jog is a different animal — lion, penguin, kangaroo, elephant. Whoever's chosen animal you are, add a tiny sound or move at the start (one hop for the kangaroo!), then settle into your smooth jog. Dad picks the animal for round 1, you pick for round 2, and so on. 🦁🐧

Your first base-building run: four gentle 3-minute jogs with 1-minute walk breaks, dressed up as a safari. Builds running endurance the smart, low-stress way — short jogs at a talk-easy pace that leave you finishing with a smile.

Speed & Fun · ~10 min

Turbo Boost Sprints & Superhero Chase

Setup: Put cones at 10, 20, and 30 yards from a start line. No ball. This is the fast, giggly part of the morning — short bursts with lots of rest, then some chase races.

How it works: First, build-up accelerations: run to the 10, then the 20, then the 30, each one starting easy and smoothly speeding up — walk all the way back between each (2–3 reps). Then one timed 20-yard dash for the record book — Dad works the timer. Finish with Superhero Chase races: the "hero" gets a head-start handicap and the other tries to catch up before the finish cone, best of a few. Log your fastest 20-yard dash time — lower is better, so it's a record to chase.

Cues: Push the ground away hard for the first few steps and stay low as you build up, then run tall at top speed. Full rest between sprints — speed work only works when you're fresh, so never rush back to the line.

🎉 Make it fun: Each of you picks a superhero for the chase and does the pose before "GO!" Give the slower-that-round runner a fair head start so it's always a photo finish, and let Dad do the dramatic slow-motion replay of the winning lunge. 🦸💨

Short build-up sprints, one timed 20-yard dash, and handicapped superhero chase races. Trains acceleration and top-end speed with plenty of rest — the fun, fast payoff after the endurance work.

Cooldown · ~10 min

Walk, Stretch & Chat

Walk it off: Easy 3–4 minute walk together until your breathing settles all the way down. Keep sipping water — it's heading for a hot, sticky day.

Stretch: Give the running muscles some love — calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, and glutes. Hold each about 20 seconds, relaxed, no bouncing.

Chat & recap: As you stretch, each name one great thing from your first run — a smooth jog, a funny animal greeting, a winning chase. Big high-five, then tap a button below so it counts. Loved a drill? Tap its ⭐! 🎉

🌧️ Rain Backup (small-space, ~60 min)

Storm rolls in early or you're stuck inside? No running room needed — a couple of square feet each is plenty. We swap the outdoor run for an in-place cardio-and-strength session that still builds the engine.

Warm-up (2 rounds of 90 sec): march in place → arm circles & trunk twists → light in-place fast feet. Rest 30 seconds between rounds.

In-place "run" intervals (4 rounds of: 2 min work + 1 min easy march): alternate each work block between steady jog-in-place and high-knees / butt-kicks in place. That's your ~8–12 minutes of "running" — count it as the day's base-builder. Chat-easy effort, quick light feet.

Strength + speed circuit (3 rounds, 40 sec on / 20 sec off), all in place: squats → push-ups (knees OK) → mountain climbers → plank hold → in-place fast feet. Rest a minute between rounds.

Finish: The normal stretch — calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, glutes, ~20 seconds each — then trade one "proud moment" and a high-five.

🎉 Make it fun: Reaction round — Dad claps and you switch instantly (one clap = fast feet, two claps = freeze in a plank). Miss a call and you owe five squats. 😄
