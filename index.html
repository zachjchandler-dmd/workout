WARM-UP · 5 MINmoving ball

Shadow Dribble — Follow the Leader

Setup: Open grass about 15 × 15 yards. One ball each (or share one and swap). No cones needed.

How it works: Son leads, dribbling anywhere he likes — curves, stops, spins. Dad follows a few steps behind, copying every path and touch with his own ball. After ~2 min, swap: Dad leads, Son shadows. Keep the ball close and moving the whole time.

Cues: Small touches, head up to watch the leader, use both feet.

Make it fun: Leader calls out silly moves — “dinosaur stomp!”, “ice-skater glide!” — and the follower has to copy it with the ball still glued to their feet. 😄

~15 × 15 yd grid — leader improvises, follower copies, then swap.

WARM-UP · 5 MINreaction / stop-start

Mirror Match Freeze

Setup: Stand facing each other about 3 yards apart, each with a ball at your feet. Two cones mark a small “goal line” each can shuffle along.

How it works: One person is the mirror-leader, gently rolling the ball left, right, forward, back with the sole of the foot. The other mirrors it like a reflection. When the leader shouts “FREEZE!” both stop dead with a foot on top of the ball. Hold 2 seconds, then go again. Swap leader every minute.

Cues: Soft knees, quick reactions, freeze totally still — no wobble.

Make it fun: Whoever wobbles or moves after “FREEZE” does one goofy celebration dance before the next round. Loser of the round picks the dance. 🕺

Face-to-face — mirror the rolls, freeze on “FREEZE!”, swap leaders each minute.

TECHNICAL · 7–8 MINfirst touch

Rebounder Wall — First-Touch Trap & Turn

Setup: Stand ~5 yards from the rebounder wall. Place two cones a couple yards to the left and right behind you as “turn targets.”

How it works: Pass firmly into the wall. As the ball rebounds, take a soft first touch out of your feet toward one of the cones, turn, and dribble two steps that way — then reset and go again. Alternate turning left and right. Do ~8 reps each, then swap with Dad.

Cues: Cushion the ball (don’t let it bounce off you), first touch out of your body, then head up.

Make it fun: Call the turn direction a split-second after the ball leaves the wall — “LEFT!” — so he has to react on the fly. Clean touch + right turn = 1 point. First to 6. 🎯

Dashed = pass & rebound · yellow = first touch + turn toward a cone.

TECHNICAL · 7–8 MINclose control

Cone Slalom Dribbling

Setup: Line up 5 cones in a row, ~1.5 yards apart. Start behind the first cone with a ball.

How it works: Weave through the cones using small inside-foot touches, keeping the ball within a step the whole way. At the end, do a quick turn and come back the other side. Both feet get used on the way down and back. 4–5 trips, resting between.

Cues: Little touches, stay close to the cones, push off the outside foot to change direction.

Make it fun: Time each run with your phone and try to beat your own best by a second — clean runs only (knock a cone = +2 sec penalty!). Dad runs it too so you’re racing the clock together. ⏱️

Small touches through the gates; turn at the end and return.

SPEED & AGILITY · 7 MINfast feet

Speed-Stick Quick Feet Ladder

Setup: Lay 5–6 speed sticks flat on the ground ~1.5 feet apart to make a ladder. A cone sits 8 yards past the last stick.

How it works: Run through the sticks with fast, light feet — one foot between each stick — then accelerate in a sprint to the far cone. Walk back to recover. Mix in a “two-feet-in each gap” variation. 5–6 rounds, alternating who goes.

Cues: Quick feet, pump the arms, chin level, then explode out of the ladder.

Make it fun: Dad calls “GO!” at a random moment while you’re bouncing on your toes at the start — reaction start! Beat Dad to the cone for the win. 🚀

Light feet through the sticks → explode into an 8-yd sprint.

SPEED & AGILITY · 8 MINchange of direction

Hurdle Hops → Reaction Cut

Setup: Place 3 trapezoid hurdles in a row ~1 yard apart. Put a cone 6 yards to the left and another 6 yards to the right of the last hurdle.

How it works: Two-footed hop over each hurdle (quick and springy). As you clear the last one, Dad points left or right — you plant and sprint to that cone. Jog back. 6 rounds, then swap roles so Dad hops and Son points.

Cues: Soft, quiet landings, arms up for balance, sharp plant-and-go on the cut.

Make it fun: Dad can throw a fake — point one way, then switch! If he fakes you out and you still cut correctly, you steal a point. 😆

White or yellow = whichever cut Dad calls after the last hop.

GAME · 15 MIN1v1 · scored

Keep-Away Kings

Setup: Make a small square with 4 cones, about 8 × 8 yards. One ball.

How it works: Son starts with the ball and tries to keep it away from Dad inside the square for 20 seconds — if he keeps it the whole time, he scores a point. If Dad wins it (or it leaves the square), Dad’s turn to keep it away. Play to 5 points. Dad plays at the son’s level — pressure enough to challenge, not to crush. Swap who starts each round.

Cues: Shield with your body, use both feet, keep your head up to see where Dad is coming from.

Make it fun: Add a “Nutmeg Bonus” — if either of you slips the ball between the other’s legs, that’s an instant point and full bragging rights! 🏆

Keeper shields the ball; defender chases — hold it 20 sec to score.

COOLDOWN · 5 MIN

Walk, Stretch & Recap

Slow walk around the yard together to let heart rates settle. Then a few easy stretches: quad pull, calf stretch, reach for toes, big arm circles — hold each ~15 sec.

Recap ritual: Dad names one specific thing the son did great today (“your first touch on the wall drill was so clean”), and the son names his favorite drill. Fist bump to finish. 🤝
