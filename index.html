Warm-up · 5 min

Rock–Paper–Scissors Chase

Setup: Two "safe" lines about 10 yards apart with a middle line between them (cones mark all three). No ball — running mornings are ball-free, and this one wakes up your reactions and your first burst of speed. Dad and Son both start at the middle line, face to face.

How it works: Play one round of rock–paper–scissors. The winner instantly chases the loser, who sprints to try to reach their own safe line before getting tagged. Tag before the line = 1 point for the chaser; reach it safe = 1 point for the runner. Jog back to the middle and go again — best of 10.

Cues: Stay on the balls of your feet so you can explode either direction. React the instant you see the throw — the first two steps win it. Run tall, pump your arms, and don't peek back while you're being chased.

🎉 Make it fun: Best throw wins a "power-up" — do a silly super-hero pose before the next round. Try a round where you throw with your feet (jump into rock/paper/scissors) for extra giggles.

Play RPS at the middle line; the winner instantly chases the loser toward a safe line 10 yards away. Trains reaction, that explosive first step, and acceleration — all with a lot of laughing.

Warm-up · 5 min

Fast-Feet Form School

Setup: Mark a lane about 15 yards long with two cones. No ball — this is a running-form warm-up that grooves good shape before you jog. You do each drill down the lane and walk back easy.

How it works: Go through the sequence one drill at a time, down and walk back: ankling (tiny quick steps popping off the toes), A-skips (skip driving one knee up), B-skips (A-skip but flick the lower leg out and paw the ground), heel flicks (butt-kicks), carioca (grapevine, both ways), straight-leg bounds (toy-soldier kicks), and finish with two build-up strides — start easy and smoothly accelerate to fast by the far cone.

Cues: Tall and light — imagine a string pulling the top of your head up. Quick feet, soft landings under your hips, arms driving from the shoulder. This isn't a race; it's teaching your legs the shape of good running.

🎉 Make it fun: Dad copies Son's form on each rep, then Son copies Dad's — call out who looked "bounciest." Nail a smooth build-up stride and you earn a slow-motion replay walk-back. 😄

A no-ball ladder of classic running-form drills — ankling, A- and B-skips, heel flicks, carioca, straight-leg bounds and build-up strides. Teaches tall, springy, efficient running shape before the jog.

Endurance · ~28 min

Trailblazer Run-Walk Loops

Setup: Pick an easy out-and-back or a loop around the yard/park with a couple of cones as landmarks. Phone or watch to keep time. This is your first run session, so we build gently — the goal is easy, chatty jogging, not fast running.

How it works: Do 3 rounds of: jog 4 minutes easy, then walk 1.5 minutes to recover. That's about 12 minutes of total jogging today — a great base-builder starting point. If 4 minutes feels tough, it's totally fine to sneak in a few extra walk steps and pick the jog back up; if it feels easy, hold the pace steady rather than speeding up. Keep it to a pace where you could chat the whole time.

Cues: Easy, even breathing — "in for two steps, out for two." Relax your shoulders and hands, short comfy strides, land softly. This is a conversation pace: if you can't talk, slow down. Sip water on every walk break.

🎉 Make it fun: Each jog is a "chapter" of an adventure you make up together — round one you're outrunning a friendly dragon, round two exploring a jungle, round three racing the sunrise home. Pick a landmark to jog to each round so it feels like exploring, not laps.

Three rounds of jog-4-min / walk-1.5-min — about 12 minutes of easy jogging to open the running column. Builds base endurance the safe way for a 10-year-old: gentle, chatty, and repeatable.

Speed · ~10 min

Flying-Start Sprints & Shadow Races

Setup: Set cones at 10, 20, and 30 yards from a start line, plus a finish cone. No ball — pure fun speed. Fully recover between every sprint (walk back slowly); short and snappy beats tired and sloppy.

How it works: Three parts. (1) Acceleration ladder — one build-up to the 10, one to the 20, one to the 30, getting a little faster each time. (2) Timed 20-yd dash — Dad times a couple of all-out 20-yard sprints and you log your fastest below. (3) Shadow & handicap races — "shadow" races where Son leads and Dad tries to mirror two steps behind (then swap), and handicap races where Dad starts a few yards back so it's neck-and-neck to the line.

Cues: Explode low out of the start, then rise up tall as you hit top speed. Drive the arms hard, punch the knees, run through the finish cone — never slow down early. Full walk-back recovery so every rep is fresh and fast.

🎉 Make it fun: Do a signature finish-line pose for a photo each round. Give the handicap races a race-caller voice ("aaaand they're off!"). Beat your own 20-yard time and you're the "Fastest in the Family" for the day. 🏅

Build-up accelerations to 10/20/30 yards, a couple of timed 20-yard dashes, and shadow & handicap races with Dad. Pure fun speed work — start power, top-end running, and a personal-best to chase.

Cooldown · ~10 min

Walk, Stretch & Chat

Walk it off: Easy 3–4 minute walk together until your breathing settles all the way down and your legs feel calm. Keep sipping water — it's heading for a hot one.

Stretch: Give the running muscles some love — calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, and glutes. Hold each about 20 seconds, relaxed, no bouncing. Legs did all the work today, so don't skip the calves and hamstrings.

Chat & recap: While you stretch, talk it over — what was the best "chapter" of the run, was the jog easier by round three, who won the most sprint races? Each name one thing you were proud of. Big high-five, then tap a button below so it counts. If a drill was a blast, tap its ⭐! 🎉

🌧️ Rain Backup (small-space, ~45 min)

Storm rolls in early or the heat's too much? No field or running room needed — a couple of square feet each is plenty. On a running day we swap the jog for in-place cardio and a bodyweight circuit so you still get the legs and lungs working.

In-place cardio warm-up (2 rounds of 90 sec): March in place → high-knee jog in place → butt-kicks in place, staying light on your toes. Rest 30 seconds between rounds.

Cardio intervals (6 rounds, 40 sec on / 30 sec off): Rotate each round — high-knees · fast feet (tiny quick steps) · jumping jacks · skater hops side to side · mountain climbers · quick step-ups on a low stair. All in place, no running room needed.

Strength circuit (2 rounds): 30 seconds each — squats · push-ups (knees OK) · plank hold · glute bridges · calf raises. Rest a minute between rounds.

Finish: The normal stretch — calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, ~20 seconds each — then trade one "great thing" and a high-five.

🎉 Make it fun: Reaction round — Dad claps and you switch moves instantly (one clap = high-knees, two claps = freeze in a plank). Miss a call and you owe five squats. 😄
