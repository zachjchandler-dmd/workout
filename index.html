Warm-up · 0–5 min

Passing-Gate Chase

Setup
Scatter 4 pairs of cones as little 1-yard "gates" around a 12×12 yard box. One ball between you.

How it works
You and your son jog around the box passing the ball back and forth. Every pass has to travel through one of the gates. Count out loud — how many clean gate-passes can you two string together in a row before a miss? Reset and try to beat it.

Cues

  • Pass with the inside of the foot, ankle locked.
  • Keep moving — don't stand still waiting for the ball.
  • Call your partner's name before you pass.
🎉 Make it fun: Every time you beat the old record, the record-breaker gets to do a silly celebration the other has to copy!

Pass through any gate while you both keep jogging around the box.

Warm-up · 5–10 min

Traffic-Light Dribble

Setup
Each of you has a ball inside the same 12×12 box. One person is the "light" caller (swap after a minute).

How it works
Both dribble around gently. Caller shouts colors: GREEN = dribble fast, YELLOW = slow tippy-taps, RED = freeze with a foot on top of the ball. Late to react? Give the caller a point. Then swap who calls.

Cues

  • Little touches — keep the ball within a step.
  • Eyes up so you can hear and see the caller.
  • On RED, stop the ball dead — no rolling away.
🎉 Make it fun: Add a surprise color — "PURPLE" means spin all the way around before freezing. Dad has to do it too!

Dribble on green, tippy-tap on yellow, freeze foot-on-ball on red.

Technical · 10–18 min

Sole-Roll & L-Turn Ball Mastery

Setup
One ball each, standing an arm's length apart so you can mirror each other. No cones needed.

How it works
Work through 4 moves, ~45 seconds each, then repeat the set once:

  • Sole rolls — roll the ball side to side with the bottom of the foot, alternating feet.
  • Toe taps — light taps on top of the ball, quick feet.
  • Inside-inside — push the ball inside-foot to inside-foot in a V.
  • L-turn — pull the ball back with the sole, then push it out sideways with the inside foot to make an "L".

Cues

  • Soft knees, on the balls of your feet.
  • Small touches — control beats speed here.
  • Dad demos slow, then both go at "game speed".
🎉 Make it fun: Son is the coach for one round — he invents a 5th move and Dad has to copy it perfectly (bloopers count double for laughs).

Mirror each other through sole rolls, taps, inside-inside, and the L-turn.

Technical · 18–25 min

Rebounder-Wall Two-Touch Passing

Setup
Stand ~5–6 yards from the rebounding wall. Place two cones as a "target gate" on the wall to aim between. One ball.

How it works
Pass firmly into the wall, take a clean first touch out of your feet, then pass again — two touches, no more. Go for 60 seconds, count your clean reps, then Dad goes. Round 2: receive with your weaker foot.

Cues

  • First touch slightly forward, into space — not stuck under you.
  • Firm pass back through the target gate.
  • Stay light on your toes, ready to move to the ball.
🎉 Make it fun: "Around the World" — after every 5 clean reps you both take one step farther from the wall. How far back can you get before it falls apart?

Pass in through the gate, one clean first touch, pass back — repeat fast.

Speed & Agility · 25–40 min

Zig-Zag Cut Course & Reaction Starts

Setup
Line up 4 speed sticks in a zig-zag (2 yards apart), then 2 trapezoid hurdles, then a cone 8 yards out as the finish. Ball waits after the course.

How it works
Two parts, alternate turns so there's always a rest:

  • Cut course: weave through the speed sticks with sharp inside/outside cuts, quick feet over the two trapezoid hurdles, then explode in an 8-yard sprint to the finish cone.
  • Reaction start: from the finish cone, wait for Dad's random call ("GO!" / a clap / "BLUE!") and sprint back to a ball, first touch and stop it. 4–5 rounds each.

Cues

  • Low and springy over the hurdles — don't stomp.
  • Push off the outside foot to change direction.
  • First two sprint steps are short and punchy, then open up.
🎉 Make it fun: Give the son a 1-second head start on every reaction race. If he beats Dad three times, Dad owes him a piggy-back lap!

Weave the poles, bounce the hurdles, sprint to finish — then react & race back to a ball.

Game · 40–55 min

Finishing Frenzy — 1v1 Attack & Score

Setup
Make a small goal with two cones (3 yards wide). Mark an attacker start 10 yards out with a cone. Rebounding wall or a spare net behind the goal to stop balls if you have one.

How it works
Son starts as attacker with the ball; Dad is a half-speed defender between him and the goal. Son tries to beat Dad with a move and score. Scoring: goal = 3 points, shot on target that Dad blocks = 1 point, nutmeg or a clean skill move = bonus 1 point. Play to 15, then swap roles. Keep it moving — quick resets, lots of shots.

Cues

  • Attack with speed, then a change of pace to get past.
  • Shoot with the laces, ankle locked, follow through at goal.
  • Pick your corner — placement beats power.
🎉 Make it fun: Commentate it like a World Cup final — big crowd noises for every goal, and the winner picks the celebration song for the walk home.

Beat the half-speed defender with a move, then finish into the cone goal.

Cooldown · 55–60 min

Walk, Stretch & High-Five

Easy walk lap to let heart rates come down, then a few gentle holds (~15 sec each): calves, quads, hamstrings, hips, and a big overhead reach.

Recap one great thing
Before you head in, Dad names one specific thing the son did really well today — a sharp cut, a firm pass, a great finish — and asks him what he was proudest of. End on a high-five.

💧 Hydrate: water during the walk, and again once you're inside.
