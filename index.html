Warm-up · 5 min

Soccer Ball Shark Attack

Setup: Make a box about 10×10 yards with a cone at each corner. Son starts inside with a ball at his feet. Dad is the "shark" (no ball).

How it works: Son dribbles all around inside the box keeping the ball under control while the shark tries to poke it out or force it over a line. Shield it with your body, use little touches, change direction. Every 45–60 seconds, freeze, catch your breath, and switch roles so Dad dribbles and Son is the shark. Fun, not full-speed — this is the warm-up.

Cues: Head up so you can see the shark coming, small quick touches, and put your body between the ball and the defender to shield.

🎉 Make it fun: Every time the shark steals it, it does a big goofy "chomp" celebration — then hands it right back. Add a rule that the dribbler can call "SHARK NAP!" once per round to make Dad freeze for three seconds.

Son keeps the ball moving and shielded inside the 10-yard box while the shark chases — swap roles every minute.

Warm-up · 5 min

Coach Says: Fancy Feet

Setup: Each of you has a ball resting on the ground in front of you. Stand a few feet apart so you can see each other. Today Son is the coach first (Kid's Choice, after all).

How it works: It's soccer Simon Says. The coach calls a move and everyone does it for ~10 seconds: toe taps (tap the top of the ball back and forth), sole rolls (roll the ball side to side with the bottom of your foot), foundations (tap the ball inside-foot to inside-foot), little jumps over the ball, spin around the ball. If the coach didn't say "Coach says," don't do it! Trade coach every minute.

Cues: Stay on your toes, keep the ball close, look up at the coach between reps. Light and quick, not stompy.

🎉 Make it fun: The coach gets to invent one totally made-up move ("helicopter toe taps!") that the other has to copy. Gotcha calls with no "Coach says" earn the caller a point.

Face off with a ball each and mirror the coach's footwork calls — but only when they say "Coach says."

Technical · ~8 min

Wall Ball First-Touch Challenge

Setup: Stand about 4–5 yards from the rebounder wall (or any flat wall). Put two cones as a little "control gate" just in front of you, a couple feet wide, where you want to settle the ball.

How it works: Pass the ball firmly into the wall, then take a soft first touch to settle the rebound inside your control gate, and pass again — smooth rhythm: pass → cushion → pass. Count how many clean controlled touches in a row you can string together without the ball getting away. Try both feet, then try a "touch across" — cushion the ball a step to the side before you pass it back.

Cues: Relax your foot on the first touch (soft ankle, like catching an egg), get your body behind the ball, and guide it — don't stab at it.

🎉 Make it fun: Play "don't break the chain" — every clean touch adds one; the first miss resets to zero. Can you two set a new house record together? Log your best streak below! 🏆

Pass the wall, cushion the rebound into your cone gate, pass again — count the clean touches in a row.

Technical · ~7 min

Panna Gate Dash

Setup: Scatter 5–6 "gates" around a 15×15 yard area — each gate is just two cones about 2 feet apart. Spread them out at random angles.

How it works: Dribble the ball and score a "panna" by pushing it cleanly through any gate. You can't use the same gate twice in a row. Count how many gates you can go through in 60 seconds. Rest, then try to beat it. Use both feet and change direction to hunt down the next gate.

Cues: Head up to spot the next gate, little push touches to steer, and accelerate through the gate rather than slowing down at it.

🎉 Make it fun: Dad calls a "bonus gate" mid-round worth double — or stands guard at one gate as a friendly obstacle son has to dribble around to reach. Whoever scores more pannas in their minute wins.

Weave the ball through the scattered two-cone gates, never the same one twice — count your pannas in a minute.

Speed & Agility · ~15 min

Champion's Agility Circuit

Setup: Build a four-station course in a line: (1) 4–5 speed sticks flat on the ground about 1.5 feet apart, (2) 2–3 trapezoid hurdles a couple feet apart, (3) 4 cones in a zig-zag to weave, (4) a finish cone about 10 yards past the last weave for an all-out sprint.

How it works: Run the whole course for time — quick feet through the sticks (one foot in each space), two-foot hops over the hurdles, weave the cones staying low, then sprint through the finish. Dad times it. Walk back to recover, then try to beat your time. Do 4–6 runs total, resting between. This is a no-ball agility block.

Cues: Fast light feet on the sticks, soft knees landing the hops, lean and push off the outside foot to cut around cones, and explode through the finish — don't slow down early.

🎉 Make it fun: Handicap race — Dad runs it too, but Son gets a head start so it's a real photo finish. Give the course a name ("The Gauntlet") and log your fastest time to chase next week. 🏁

Rip through all four stations for time — quick feet, hops, weave, then an all-out sprint through the finish cone.

Game · ~15 min

1v1 World Cup Shootout

Setup: Make two small goals about 4 feet wide with cones, roughly 12–15 yards apart. One ball. (No keepers — the goals are small, so accuracy matters.)

How it works: Straight 1v1 — win the ball, attack either goal, defend both. First to a goal wins the point; loser restarts with the ball at their goal. First to 5 wins the "World Cup." Play a best-of-three matches. Keep it honest and call your own fouls with a smile. Announce it like a big match — "and it's the FINAL!"

Cues: On attack, use a quick change of direction to get past; on defense, stay goal-side, be patient, and poke the ball when they touch it too far.

🎉 Make it fun: Son gets to pick the "teams" (he's Brazil, Dad's… whoever gets to lose 😄) and do a full celebration slide after every goal. Golden-goal overtime if it ends 4–4.

Two mini goals, one ball, straight 1v1 — win possession and score in either net; first to five takes the cup.

Cooldown · ~5 min

Walk, Stretch & Recap

Walk it off: An easy lap or two together until your breathing settles — no rush, sip some water.

Stretch: Calves, quads, hamstrings, hips, and a gentle shoulder roll — hold each about 20 seconds. Lots of quick feet today, so give those legs some love.

Recap one great thing: Since it's your day, buddy — you go first. Each of you names one awesome thing the other did (a nutmeg, a fast circuit, a great first touch). Finish with a high-five and a big drink of water.

🌧️ Rain Backup (small-space, ~40 min)

Storm rolls in or the heat's just too much? No running room needed — a couple of square feet each is plenty. We keep it fun and Kid's-Choice-flavored indoors, with the ball at your feet the whole time.

Ball mastery ladder (2 rounds, 40 sec each, tiny space): toe taps · sole rolls side-to-side · foundations (inside-inside) · sole-roll pull-backs · around-the-ball taps. Count your toe taps in the last 30 seconds and try to beat it.

Strength & hops circuit (2 rounds): 30 seconds each — squats · push-ups (knees OK) · plank hold · standing mountain climbers · little two-foot jumps. Rest a minute between rounds.

Mini panna game: Set one narrow cone gate a few feet away and take turns doing 60 seconds of controlled dribble-taps through it and back — most clean passes wins.

Finish: The normal stretch — calves, quads, hamstrings, hips, ~20 seconds each — then trade one "great thing" and a high-five.

🎉 Make it fun: Sole-roll showdown — both count sole rolls for 30 seconds and try to beat your own number. Winner picks the next move in the circuit.
