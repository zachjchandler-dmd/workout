Warm-up · 5 min

Stuck-in-the-Mud Dribble

Setup: Mark a box about 12×12 yards with 4 cones. Each of you has a ball inside the box. It's a soccer day, so the ball is in from the very first minute.

How it works: One person is "It" (start with Dad) dribbling their ball while trying to tag the other, who is also dribbling. Get tagged and you're "stuck in the mud" — freeze with your foot on top of your ball. To get un-stuck, the free player dribbles a full circle around you. Play 60–90 second rounds, then switch who's It. Keep heads up and the ball close.

Cues: Small touches with the laces and inside of the foot, eyes up to spot the tagger, use your body to shield the ball while you turn away from pressure.

🎉 Make it fun: Add a "rescue countdown" — if you free your partner you both yell "UNSTUCK!" and get a point. Dad can play It on one foot for a round to even it up. 😄

A dribble-tag game where a tag freezes you on your ball and a partner's lap frees you. Sneaks in tons of close-control dribbling, shielding and head-up awareness while it just feels like tag.

Warm-up · 5 min

Superhero Mobility Moves

Setup: No ball for this one. Mark a lane about 12–15 yards with a cone at each end. This is a movement warm-up that gets the hips, legs and back loose and springy before the ball skills.

How it works: Travel down the lane with one move, walk back, then the next — like a superhero training montage: inchworm walkouts → Spider-Man lunges (elbow to the ground) → walking leg swings (kick to your hand) → skips for height → carioca (grapevine) side steps → toy-soldier straight-leg kicks. One length each, about 5 minutes.

Cues: Move smooth and controlled, reach through the full range, stand tall between moves. This wakes the body up — it's not a race.

🎉 Make it fun: Each of you picks a superhero and does the moves "in character" — Dad guesses who you are from how you move. Best superhero landing pose at the far cone wins the round. 🦸

A no-ball dynamic warm-up — inchworms, Spider-Man lunges, leg swings, skips, carioca and toy soldiers, one length at a time. Loosens hips and hamstrings and fires up the body before the ball work.

Technical · ~7 min

V-Pull Dribble Gates

Setup: Set 4–5 little 1-yard cone "gates" scattered a few yards apart. One ball. The move today is the pull-back V: drag the ball back with the sole of your foot, then push it out at an angle with the inside of the same foot — the ball traces a "V."

How it works: Dribble to a gate, do a clean pull-back V right in the gate to change direction, then attack the next gate. Hit as many gates as you can with a clean V in 60 seconds — that's your number to log. Then Son and Dad switch. Do 2–3 timed rounds, resetting between.

Cues: Sole on top to drag it back (don't step on it), then a quick inside-foot push to explode out. Little touches, and accelerate out of the V like you just beat a defender.

🎉 Make it fun: Name each gate after a defender you "juke." Every clean V earns a point; a sloppy one where the ball gets away costs you a "do-over" gate. Beat your own count each round! ⚡

Dribble gate to gate doing a clean pull-back V at each one to change direction, counting clean gates in 60 seconds. Builds sole control, sharp direction changes and confident close dribbling.

Technical · ~8 min

Wall Volley Rally

Setup: Stand 3–4 yards from the rebounder wall with one ball. This drill is about keeping a controlled rally going off the wall without letting the ball run away.

How it works: Pass the ball firmly into the wall, take a clean first touch to settle the rebound, then pass it back — over and over, counting how many clean controlled touches in a row you keep the rally alive in 60 seconds (your longest streak is the number to log). Mix it up as you get comfortable: one round right foot only, one round weak foot, one round two-touch (control + pass).

Cues: Get behind the ball, cushion the first touch — "soft foot," like catching an egg — then a firm, accurate pass with the inside of the foot. Stay on your toes and ready to move to the ball.

🎉 Make it fun: Call your rally like a commentator — "5… 6… 7 in a row!" Dad tries to beat your best streak, and the loser has to do the winner's silliest celebration. 📣

A controlled first-touch rally off the rebounder wall, counting your longest streak of clean touches in 60 seconds. Sharpens first touch, passing accuracy and quick feet to the ball.

Speed & Agility · ~15 min

Whirlwind Agility Circuit

Setup: No ball — pure footwork and speed. Build a 4-station circuit in a line: (1) speed-stick ladder flat on the ground → (2) 3 trapezoid hurdles to hop over → (3) 4 cones in a zig-zag to weave → (4) a 15-yard sprint through a finish cone. Phone/watch to time it.

How it works: Go through all four stations as one flowing run — quick feet through the ladder, snappy two-foot hops over the hurdles, tight cuts weaving the cones, then explode on the sprint. Dad times the whole circuit; that's your number to log. Rest fully (about a minute) and go again 3–4 times, trying to beat your time.

Cues: Fast light feet in the ladder (quality over speed at first), land soft and springy off the hurdles, stay low and drive off the outside foot on each cone cut, then run through the finish — don't slow up early.

🎉 Make it fun: It's a "beat the clock" grand prix — Son and Dad each get a lap and Dad runs it backward through the cones as a handicap. Announce the leaderboard after every run. 🏎️

A four-station speed circuit — ladder, hurdles, zig-zag weave, sprint — timed end to end. Builds quick feet, spring, sharp cuts and acceleration, with a circuit time to beat each round.

Game · ~15 min

Around-the-World Shooting

Setup: Set up a goal (two cones or the rebounder as the target). Mark 5 numbered shooting spots in an arc around the goal — some straight on, some at angles — about 8–12 yards out. A little pile of balls at each spot, or fetch between shots.

How it works: "Around the World" — you must score from spot 1, then 2, then 3, 4, and 5 in order to travel all the way around. Miss and you stay on that spot for your next turn. Take turns with Dad; first to complete all 5 spots wins the round. Log how many of the 5 spots you hit on your best trip around.

Cues: Plant your non-kicking foot beside the ball pointing at the target, strike through the middle with your laces (or inside foot for placement), head steady and eyes on the ball at contact. Pick your corner before you shoot.

🎉 Make it fun: Call your shot like a pro — "top corner, spot 3!" Nail a called corner and it counts as two spots. Dad shoots with his weak foot to keep the race close. 🥅

Score from five numbered spots around the goal in order to travel "around the world," logging your best spots-hit out of 5. A shooting-and-finishing game that rewards accuracy from every angle.

Cooldown · ~5 min

Walk, Stretch & Recap

Walk it off: Easy 2–3 minute walk together, gently dribbling the ball at a stroll, until your breathing settles. Keep sipping water — it's heading for a hot one.

Stretch: Give the legs some love — calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, and glutes. Hold each about 20 seconds, relaxed, no bouncing.

Recap: Each name one great thing from today — a slick V-pull, your longest wall rally, a fast circuit lap, or a top-corner finish. Big high-five, then tap a button below so it counts. Loved a drill? Tap its ⭐! 🎉

🌧️ Rain Backup (small-space, ~60 min)

Storm rolls in early or you're stuck inside? No field or running room needed — a couple of square feet each is plenty. We keep the ball touches going with in-place foot skills and add a bodyweight circuit so it still counts as your soccer-day workout.

Warm-up (2 rounds of 90 sec): March in place → in-place toe taps on top of the ball → jumping jacks, light and quick. Rest 30 seconds between rounds.

Ball mastery (6 rounds, 40 sec on / 20 sec off), all on the spot: toe taps · foot rolls side-to-side (sole) · inside-inside taps (bell) · pull-push (sole back, laces forward) · foundations (inside-foot taps) · sole rolls with a pull-back V in place. Count your best 30 seconds of toe taps if you want a number for the record book.

Strength circuit (2 rounds): 30 seconds each — squats · push-ups (knees OK) · plank hold · glute bridges · calf raises. Rest a minute between rounds.

Finish: The normal stretch — calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, glutes, ~20 seconds each — then trade one "proud moment" and a high-five.

🎉 Make it fun: Reaction round — Dad claps and you switch skills instantly (one clap = toe taps, two claps = freeze with your foot on the ball). Miss a call and you owe five squats. 😄
