Warm-up · 5 min

Treasure Grab Dribble

Setup: Make a box about 12×12 yards with four corner cones. Scatter 6–8 "treasure" cones (or bibs) in the middle. You each have a ball and start dribbling around the outside edge of the box.

How it works: Keep your ball moving around the outside. When Dad calls a color or a number — "GO, blue!" — both of you dribble into the middle, tap one matching treasure cone with your foot (ball still under control!), then dribble back out to the edge before the next call. React fast, but never leave your ball behind. Go for about 5 minutes, counting how many treasures you each bank.

Cues: Little touches with the laces and insides to keep the ball close, head up to spot the treasure and each other, and change direction sharply on the call. Shield the ball if you two cross paths.

🎉 Make it fun: Give the treasures silly names ("golden banana!") and keep a pirate tally — most treasures banked wins bragging rights. Sneak in a fake call ("GO… gorilla!") and anyone who dashes in owes a pirate "arrr!" 🏴‍☠️⚽

A reaction dribble game — read the call, cut into traffic, tap a target, and get back out with the ball glued to your feet. Warms the body up while sharpening close control, awareness, and quick direction changes, all as a treasure hunt.

Warm-up · 5 min

Adventure Trail Mobility

Setup: No ball for this one. Set two cones about 15 yards apart to make a "trail." Each move is a station along an imaginary adventure trail — you travel down doing the move, then walk back easy for the next one.

How it works: Work down the trail: "over the logs" = high-knee marches → "under the branches" = walking lunge with a reach → "crawl through the cave" = inchworms → "cross the river" = side shuffles (both ways) → "climb the hill" = carioca (grapevine) → "march to the summit" = toy-soldier straight-leg kicks. Finish with one relaxed skip down the trail. About 5 minutes — smooth and loose, waking up hips, ankles, and legs before the ball work.

Cues: Move with control, not speed. Reach tall on the marches, sink the lunges gently, and keep the core switched on through the inchworms. Breathe easy — this is the "get loose" part.

🎉 Make it fun: Narrate the adventure out loud together and add sound effects — creaky logs, splashy river, echoey cave. Dad leads one length, Son leads the next, and each leader gets to invent one bonus "trail obstacle." 🏕️🌲

A no-ball dynamic-mobility warm-up dressed up as an adventure trail. Opens the hips, ankles, and legs and switches the core on, prepping the body for sharp cuts and sprints without any pounding.

Technical · ~7 min

Fake-Out Feint Gates

Setup: Build 4 cone "gates" (two cones ~1 yard apart) in a line, about 4–5 yards between gates. You've got a ball and start at the first gate. This is a brand-new move: the body feint (shoulder drop) to make a defender think you're going one way.

How it works: Dribble toward a gate. Just before it, drop your shoulder and lean/step as if you're going left, then quickly push the ball out the other way with the outside of your right foot and burst through the gate. Reset and do the next gate the opposite direction. The fake is in your body — a defender at the gate should buy the lean. Timed challenge: how many clean feint gates can you do in 60 seconds? Log it below.

Cues: Sell the lie — bend the knee and dip the shoulder like you mean it, keep the ball close before the push, then explode the other way with a sharp outside-foot touch. Small fake, fast go.

🎉 Make it fun: Dad stands loosely at a gate as a "statue defender" — if your feint makes him lean the wrong way, that gate is worth double. Give the move a name-drop shout ("this way… NOPE!") every time you go. 🕺⚽

A first go at the body feint (shoulder drop) — dip one way, push out the other. Teaches deception and a quick outside-foot exit, the move that makes a defender freeze while you slip past.

Technical · ~7 min

Rebounder Rhythm Two-Foot Rally

Setup: Stand about 3–4 yards from the rebounding wall with your ball. This one builds a steady rhythm using both feet — right foot, left foot, right foot — passing and controlling in a smooth beat.

How it works: Pass the ball into the wall with your right foot, take the rebound with a soft first touch, then pass it back with your left foot, control, and keep alternating R–L–R–L in a rhythm. Firm, accurate passes; cushion each return so the next pass is easy. Timed challenge: how many clean alternating-foot passes can you string together in 60 seconds? Log your best below.

Cues: Open the passing foot and strike through the middle of the ball, cushion the first touch out in front where you can hit it, and stay light on your toes finding a steady beat. Weak foot counts too — that's the point!

🎉 Make it fun: Add a beat — count out loud or clap a rhythm, "right-left-right-left." See if you can keep the rally going while Dad names a color each pass. Beat your 60-second count and it's a mini record celebration. 🎵⚽

A both-feet passing rally off the wall in a steady rhythm. Sharpens firm passing, a clean first touch, and — the big win — weak-foot confidence, with a 60-second count to chase each week.

Speed & Agility · ~15 min

Cyclone Agility Circuit

Setup: Line up a quick course (no ball): speed-stick ladder → two trapezoid hurdles → a 4-cone box (about 5×5 yards) → a 12-yard sprint finish. Set it so one lap flows straight through.

How it works: Go one at a time and rip through the whole "cyclone": lateral in-in-out-out through the ladder → side-hops over the two trapezoid hurdles → around the 4-cone box (sprint the first side, shuffle the second, backpedal the third, shuffle the fourth) → then explode into the 12-yard sprint finish. Walk back to recover, then go again. Aim for 5–6 clean laps. Time a lap and log your fastest clean one below.

Cues: Quick, light feet in the ladder (don't look down), snappy knees over the hurdles, stay low with short choppy steps around the box, and drive hard through the finish line — don't slow up early. Full rest between laps so every lap is fast.

🎉 Make it fun: Dad runs it too and you compare times — with a fair handicap so it's a real race. Give each station a storm sound ("wind… thunder… GO!") and ring an imaginary bell when someone sets a new fastest lap. ⚡🌀

A flowing speed-and-agility circuit — ladder quickness, hurdle spring, multi-direction box work, and an all-out sprint finish. Builds fast feet, sharp changes of direction, and acceleration, with a lap time to beat every soccer week.

Game · ~15 min

Four-Goal Frenzy (1v1)

Setup: Make a box about 12×12 yards with a small goal (2 cones, ~1.5 yards wide) on each of the four sides. One ball, you vs. Dad, 1v1.

How it works: Whoever has the ball can score in ANY of the four goals — dribble through it under control for a point. The defender has to protect all four, so the trick is to fake toward one goal, then break to another (perfect spot for that new feint!). After a goal or a ball out, restart from the middle. Play games to 5, or count total goals over ~15 minutes. Keep score — this one's for real bragging rights.

Cues: Head up to see which goals are open, use those quick changes of direction from the Cyclone circuit, shield the ball when Dad pressures, and pick your moment to attack the far goal. On defense, stay goal-side and patient.

🎉 Make it fun: Name the four goals (North/South/East/West, or team names) and call a "double-points goal" each round that switches every game. Big commentary on every score, and a made-up trophy lift for the winner. 🏆⚽

A 1v1 game with four goals to attack and defend — decision-making, deception, and shielding under real pressure, in a fast, competitive format that lets today's new feint shine. Keep score for goals or games won.

Cooldown · ~5 min

Walk, Stretch & Recap

Walk it off: Easy 2–3 minute walk together, ball tucked under an arm, until your breathing settles. Keep sipping water — even a cloudy morning here gets sticky.

Stretch: Give the legs some love — calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, and glutes. Roll the ankles out after all that quick footwork. Hold each about 20 seconds, relaxed, no bouncing.

Recap: As you stretch, each name one great thing from today — a feint that fooled Dad, a smooth wall rally, a sneaky Four-Goal winner. Big high-five, then tap a button below so your session counts. Loved a drill? Tap its ⭐! 🎉

🌧️ Rain Backup (small-space, ~50 min)

Storms roll in or the grass is soaked? No running room needed — a couple of square feet each is plenty. We keep the ball skills and the sweat going with in-place ball mastery and a bodyweight circuit instead of the field session.

Warm-up (2 rounds of 90 sec): march in place → arm circles & trunk twists → light in-place fast feet. Rest 30 seconds between rounds.

Ball mastery in place (5 min, ball stays under you): toe taps → sole rolls side to side → foundation inside-inside taps → sole-roll pull-backs. 30 seconds each, twice through — quick, controlled touches in one spot.

Strength & quickness circuit (3 rounds, 30 sec on / 30 sec off), all in place: squats → push-ups → in-place fast feet → plank hold → mountain climbers. Rest a minute between rounds.

Finish: The normal stretch — calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, glutes, ~20 seconds each — then trade one "proud moment" and a high-five.

🎉 Make it fun: Reaction round — Dad claps and you switch instantly (one clap = fast feet on the ball, two claps = freeze in a plank). Miss a call and you owe five squats. 😄
