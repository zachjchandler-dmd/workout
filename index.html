Warm-up · 5 min

Crocodile River Dribble

Setup: Mark a "river" about 12 yards wide with two cone lines for the banks. Son starts on one bank with a ball; Dad is the crocodile in the middle of the river (no ball), allowed to slide side to side along one line.

How it works: On "Go!", Son dribbles across the river bank to bank while the crocodile slides along his line and tries to poke the ball out (feet only, gentle). Make it safely across = 1 gem. Every clean crossing, widen the croc's reach a little. Dad can shout "Freeze!" mid-river — stop the ball dead under your sole instantly, then "Go!" to finish. Swap roles so Dad dribbles too.

Cues: Head up to see the crocodile, ball in tight little touches, use your body to shield it. Change speed and direction to escape — push into the space the croc just left.

🎉 Make it fun: Give the crocodile a big hungry voice. Collect 5 gems and you've officially "beaten the croc" — then it's Dad's turn to dribble while you play the snappy crocodile. 🐊

Dribble a ball across a 12-yard "river" while Dad the crocodile slides along his line trying to knock it out. Trains close control, shielding, and quick reactions under pressure — with a lot of giggling.

Warm-up · 5 min

Dynamic Warm-Up Dice

Setup: No ball for this one — it's a dynamic mobility warm-up that gets your hips, hamstrings, and ankles ready to cut and sprint. Grab a die (or just call a random number 1–6) and give yourself about 10 yards of open space.

How it works: Roll or call a number and do that move down-and-back (or ~25 seconds): 1 = high-knee marches into skips, 2 = walking lunges with a torso twist, 3 = inchworm walk-outs, 4 = leg swings (front-to-back, then side-to-side), 5 = lateral shuffles & carioca, 6 = arm circles into toy-soldier kicks. Take turns rolling and do about 6–8 rolls so you cover the whole body.

Cues: Smooth and controlled, big full range — this is about waking the muscles up, not going fast. Stay tall and balanced through each move.

🎉 Make it fun: Roll the same number twice in a row and Dad has to do that move in slow-motion. Anyone who calls "7" gets to invent a wildcard move for both of you. 🎲

A roll of the die picks a dynamic movement each round — marches, lunge-twists, inchworms, leg swings, carioca and toy soldiers. A no-ball, full-body warm-up that primes the legs for cutting and sprinting.

Technical · ~8 min

Step-Over Slalom

Setup: Put 5–6 cones in a line about 1.5 yards apart to make a slalom. Ball at the start line.

How it works: Dribble through the gates doing a step-over at each cone — swing one foot around the front of the ball without touching it, then push the ball on to the next cone with the outside of the other foot. Down and back is one lap. Count your clean step-over gates in 60 seconds, alternating which foot does the step-over.

Cues: Sell the fake — a big, convincing swing right over the top of the ball, then explode out the other side. Small touches between cones, head up so you can see the whole slalom.

🎉 Make it fun: Pick a "magic word" — when Dad shouts it mid-slalom, do a double step-over at the next cone. Then try to beat your gate count from the round before. 🌀

Weave a ball through a line of cones performing a step-over at every gate, alternating feet. Builds the step-over move, close control at speed, and both-footed confidence — counted against the clock.

Technical · ~7 min

First-Touch-to-Space Wall Drill

Setup: Stand about 4–5 yards from the rebounder wall with a target cone a couple of yards to your left and another to your right.

How it works: Pass firmly into the wall, then take your first touch out to the side toward a target cone — pushing the ball into space with the inside or outside of your foot instead of letting it die under you — then set your feet and pass again off the new angle. Alternate left-space and right-space touches. Count clean directional first touches (ball ends up near the cone, under control) in 60 seconds.

Cues: Get your body behind the ball, then "cushion and redirect" in one touch — soft ankle, open your hips toward the space. Your first touch is really a little pass to yourself.

🎉 Make it fun: Name your cones "east base" and "west base" — Dad calls which base to hit as the ball comes back off the wall, so your first touch has to obey the call. Chase your best clean-touch count. 🧭

Pass off the rebounder, then take your first touch sideways into space toward a target cone — left, then right. Grooves a purposeful, directional first touch instead of a dead one, counted in 60 seconds.

Speed & Agility · ~15 min

Boomerang Agility Circuit

Setup: Lay out a boomerang-shaped course: the speed sticks (ladder) first, then two trapezoid hurdles, then three cones in a zig-zag, then a finish cone about 10 yards out for a sprint. Phone/watch for timing.

How it works: One timed run through the whole course: (1) quick feet through the ladder — one foot in each rung, (2) two-footed hops over both hurdles, (3) zig-zag weave cutting tight around the three cones, (4) explode into a 10-yard sprint through the finish. Dad times it. Rest fully, then go again 4–5 times, chasing your fastest.

Cues: Fast and light on the ladder; snappy hops, land soft and spring straight up; stay low and lean into your cuts around the cones; then run tall and drive all the way through the finish — no slowing early.

🎉 Make it fun: Dad runs it too for a "family record" board, and you call the times out like a race announcer. Beat your own fastest circuit and you're the day's Agility Champ. 🏅

A single timed course — quick feet through the ladder, hops over the hurdles, a tight zig-zag weave, then a 10-yard sprint finish. Trains foot speed, change of direction, and acceleration, with a time to beat each round.

Game · ~15 min

King of the Ring

Setup: Make a ring about 10×10 yards with cones. Both players have a ball inside the ring.

How it works: Each of you dribbles and shields your own ball while trying to kick the other person's ball out of the ring — feet only, no grabbing. If your ball gets knocked out (or you dribble it out yourself), the other player scores a point and you both reset. First to 5 wins the crown. Too easy? Play the one-ball version: 1v1 keep-away where you shield and keep possession for a 10-count to score.

Cues: Keep your body between Dad and your ball, ball on your far foot. Head up to spot him coming, and use a quick change of direction to escape pressure while you hunt for a poke at his ball.

🎉 Make it fun: The winner wears an imaginary crown and picks the victory celebration. Tie it up and play a "sudden-death" final point for the championship. 👑

1v1 in a coned ring: shield your own ball while trying to knock the other's out. A high-energy, whole-body game for close control, shielding, awareness, and reaction — first to 5 wears the crown.

Cooldown · ~5 min

Walk, Stretch & Recap

Walk it off: Easy 2–3 minute walk together until your breathing settles and your legs feel calm. Keep sipping water — it's heading for a hot one.

Stretch: Give the working muscles some love — calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, and groin (soccer loves the groin and hips). Hold each about 20 seconds, relaxed, no bouncing.

Recap one great thing: While you stretch, each name one thing you did well today — a nasty step-over, a first touch that landed perfectly, a circuit personal best, or a King-of-the-Ring steal. Big high-five, then tap a button below so it counts. Loved a drill? Tap its ⭐! 🎉

🌧️ Rain Backup (small-space, ~60 min)

Storm rolls in early or the heat's just too much? No field or running room needed — a couple of square feet each is plenty. We keep the ball on the floor for tight in-place touches and add a bodyweight circuit so you still get your feet and your legs working.

Warm-up (2 rounds of 90 sec): March in place → jumping jacks → in-place toe taps on top of the ball, light on your toes. Rest 30 seconds between rounds.

Ball mastery in place (5 min, both feet): Toe taps · sole rolls side to side · foundations (inside-inside taps) · pull-back & push — the ball barely leaves your feet, so it fits any small space. Count your fastest 30 seconds of toe taps if you want a number for the record book.

Cardio intervals (6 rounds, 40 sec on / 30 sec off): Rotate — high-knees · fast feet · skater hops side to side · mountain climbers · quick step-ups on a low stair · squat jumps. All in place.

Strength circuit (2 rounds): 30 seconds each — squats · push-ups (knees OK) · plank hold · glute bridges · calf raises. Rest a minute between rounds.

Finish: The normal stretch — calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, groin, ~20 seconds each — then trade one "great thing" and a high-five.

🎉 Make it fun: Reaction round — Dad claps and you switch moves instantly (one clap = fast feet on the ball, two claps = freeze in a plank). Miss a call and you owe five squats. 😄
