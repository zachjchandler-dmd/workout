Warm-up · 5 min

Sharks & Minnows

Setup: Mark a channel about 15 yards wide with cones — two "safe" end lines and open grass in the middle. Son (the minnow) starts on one end line with a ball at his feet; Dad (the shark) starts in the middle with no ball. Ball's on today — it's a soccer morning — so this warms up the feet and the eyes.

How it works: On "Sharks!", the minnow tries to dribble across to the far safe line while the shark tries to kick the ball out of the channel (no grabbing, just clean pokes). Make it across with your ball = 1 point. Lose your ball out = you become a helper shark on the next run. Swap roles every couple of runs so Dad dribbles too. Quick, giggly rounds.

Cues: Head up so you can see the shark coming, small touches to keep the ball close, and change speed — slow to make him commit, then burst past into the space. Shield the ball with your body when the shark is on your strong side.

🎉 Make it fun: Do a shark-fin arm on your head while you dribble. Every clean crossing earns a "chomp-proof" point; get 3 in a row and you unlock a victory dance. Try one crossing left-foot-only.

Dribble across an open channel while the shark in the middle tries to poke your ball out. Warms up close control, head-up awareness, and change of speed — the core of beating a defender.

Warm-up · 5 min

Movement Medley Relay

Setup: Mark a lane about 15 yards long with two cones. No ball for this one — it's a moving warm-up to fire up the legs and hips after the shark game. You travel down the lane with one movement, then come back with another.

How it works: Take turns picking the movement each lap and race it relay-style, down and back: high skips, side shuffles (both directions), karaoke/grapevine, backpedal, walking lunges with a reach, and finish with two build-up strides (start easy, accelerate to fast by the far cone). Add a reaction twist: when the caller yells "FREEZE!", stop dead in an athletic stance for a beat, then go.

Cues: Tall posture, arms driving, land soft and springy. This isn't a max sprint — it's waking up the body and grooving good running shape before the sharp stuff.

🎉 Make it fun: Loser of each relay lap picks the next silly movement (crab walk? bunny hops?). Nail a clean freeze the instant it's called and you steal a "focus point" from Dad.

A no-ball relay of running-shape movements down and back — skips, shuffles, karaoke, lunges, build-up strides — with a reaction freeze mixed in. Loosens hips and legs and grooves good form for the sharp work ahead.

Technical · ~7 min

Box Cut Dribble

Setup: Build a small square, about 5×5 yards, with a cone at each corner. Each player has a ball. This is a close-control move-maker: you dribble around the outside of the box and make a sharp cut at every corner using the inside and outside of your foot.

How it works: Dribble along one side of the box, and at each corner cut the ball 90° with the outside of your foot going one way, then use the inside to steady it onto the next side. Keep the ball inside the corners the whole way — one lap = all four corners. Go both directions (clockwise and counter). Count clean laps in 60 seconds and log it below.

Cues: Little touches, ball glued to your foot. Get your body around the ball to turn, look up between corners, and push out of each cut with pace. Clean beats fast — smooth first, speed later.

🎉 Make it fun: Each corner is a "defender" you just beat — call out a move name as you cut it (outside! inside! chop!). Race Dad lap-for-lap, or see who can rack up the most clean laps in a minute.

Dribble the outside of a small square, cutting the ball 90° at each corner with the outside then inside of the foot. Builds tight control, real cutting turns, and both feet under a little time pressure.

Technical · ~8 min

Rebounder Air Control

Setup: Stand about 4–5 yards from the rebounder wall with a ball. One player works while the other counts and cheers, then swap. This one's about taming a ball that comes back in the air — the touch that turns a bouncing ball into a settled one.

How it works: Toss or chip the ball up into the wall so it rebounds back off the ground or through the air. As it comes to you, cushion it with a soft first touch — top of the foot, inside of the foot, or thigh — so it drops dead at your feet within one step. Settle it, pick it up, and go again. Count clean air-controls in 60 seconds. Try a set foot-only, then a set allowing the thigh.

Cues: Watch the ball all the way onto your foot. Relax and "give" with the surface as it arrives — meet the ball and pull back slightly so it loses its bounce. Get your body behind the flight and stay light on your toes.

🎉 Make it fun: "Dead in one" challenge — if the ball settles within one step it's a point; if it bounces away, reset to zero. How high can the streak climb? Add a thigh-then-foot combo for a bonus point.

Chip the ball into the wall and cushion the airborne rebound dead with a soft first touch — foot or thigh. Builds the calm, "give-with-it" touch that controls a bouncing ball under pressure.

Speed & Agility · ~15 min

Star Reaction Circuit

Setup: Lay the speed sticks flat as a short ladder for the entry. A few steps past them, set a center cone with 4 cones around it like a star (front, back, left, right), about 4 yards out each. Put a trapezoid hurdle just past the star as a finishing hop. No ball — this is pure quickness and reaction.

How it works: Fast-feet through the ladder to the center cone, then Dad calls a point of the star — "front!" sprint out and back, "right!" side-shuffle out and back, "back!" backpedal out and back, "left!" shuffle out and back — always returning to the center before the next call. After four calls, hop the hurdle and sprint 5 yards through a finish line. Time the whole run and go 4–6 rounds, walking back to recover. Log your fastest clean run.

Cues: React on the word — explode the first step. Get low to change direction, plant the outside foot, and always face forward at the center so you can read the next call. Snappy feet on the ladder, soft landing off the hurdle.

🎉 Make it fun: Dad does a game-show voice and can throw a fake-out call ("friiiii—LEFT!"). Beat your own best time and earn a superhero landing at the finish. Handicap it so you and Dad finish neck-and-neck.

A timed reaction circuit — ladder fast-feet into a four-cone star where Dad calls the direction (sprint / shuffle / backpedal), then a hurdle hop and a sprint finish. Trains quickness, change of direction, and reading a cue on the fly.

Game · ~15 min

1v1 Gates Game

Setup: Scatter 4 small gates (two cones about 2 feet apart) around a 20×20-yard area. One ball. It's a 1v1: you both attack and defend, and you score a different way than usual — by dribbling under control through any gate.

How it works: Whoever has the ball tries to dribble cleanly through any gate to score a point; the other defends and tries to win it, then attacks the gates themselves. You can't score the same gate twice in a row — you have to find a new one, so it's all about head-up dribbling and quick changes of direction. First to 5 wins the match, then reset and play again.

Cues: Keep your head up to spot the open gate, use the moves from this morning (box cut!) to lose the defender, and shield the ball with your body when pressured. Defending — stay low, be patient, guard the nearest gates and don't dive in.

🎉 Make it fun: A "nutmeg gate" (dribble it through Dad's own legs) counts double. Do a signature celebration for every score, and let Son name each gate after a favorite player or country.

A 1v1 where you score by dribbling under control through any open gate — and never the same one twice running. The most fun way to use the morning's moves in a real, head-up battle.

Cooldown · 5 min

Walk, Stretch & Recap

Walk it off: Easy lap or two walking together until your breathing settles and you've cooled down from the gate battles. Keep sipping water — it's a hot one coming.

Stretch: Give the legs some love — calves, quads, hamstrings, hips, and a gentle groin/butterfly stretch (lots of cutting and shuffling today). Hold each about 20 seconds, relaxed, no bouncing.

Recap one great thing: While you stretch, each name your favorite moment — a clean box cut, a ball settled dead out of the air, a fast star run, a nutmeg gate. Big high-five, then tap a button below so it counts. And if a drill was a blast, tap its ⭐! 🎉

🌧️ Rain Backup (small-space, ~45 min)

Storm rolls in early or the heat's too much? No field or running room needed — a couple of square feet each is plenty. We keep the ball skills that fit a tight space and add a bodyweight circuit for the energy.

Ball mastery in place (warm-up, 2 rounds of 90 sec): With the ball right at your feet — toe taps, then sole rolls side to side, then inside-outside taps. Quick light touches, head up. Rest 30 seconds between.

Foot-skill intervals (5 rounds, 40 sec on / 30 sec off): Rotate each round — fast toe taps · sole roll + tap · foundation (inside-inside) taps · pull-back V's · outside-inside cuts on the spot. All in place, ball never leaving your little square.

Strength circuit (2 rounds): 30 seconds each — squats · push-ups (knees OK) · plank hold · mountain climbers · glute bridges. Rest a minute between rounds.

Finish: The normal stretch — calves, quads, hamstrings, hips, ~20 seconds each — then trade one "great thing" and a high-five.

🎉 Make it fun: Reaction round — Dad claps and you switch foot-skill instantly (one clap = toe taps, two claps = freeze with your foot on the ball). Miss a call and you owe five squats. 😄
