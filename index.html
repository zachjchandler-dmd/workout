Warm-up · 5 min

Compass Scramble

Setup: Put one cone in the middle and four cones around it about 6–7 yards out — one "North," one "South," one "East," one "West." Both of you start standing on the middle cone. No ball — this one's all reaction and quick feet.

How it works: Dad calls a direction and a way to move: "North — sprint!", "East — side shuffle!", "South — skip!", "West — backpedal!" You dash to that cone, tap it, and jog back to the middle ready for the next call. Fire off calls every few seconds for about 90 seconds, rest, then let Son be the caller for a round.

Cues: Explode on the call, stay light and on the balls of your feet, and get low to change direction. Face the caller so your ears do the work and your feet answer fast.

🎉 Make it fun: Throw in fake-outs — "North… NO, South!" Last one to tap the right cone does a silly wiggle. Add a color-coded round where each cone gets a color name instead of a compass point.

Start in the middle; on Dad's call, dash to that compass cone the way he says (sprint/shuffle/skip/backpedal) and jog home. Reaction, acceleration, and change of direction to wake the body up.

Warm-up · 5 min

Track Star Form Drills

Setup: Mark a short lane about 15 yards long with two cones. No ball — this is a running-form tune-up. You'll go down the lane doing one drill, then jog back easy and start the next.

How it works: Move through these one at a time, ~15 yards each: ankling (tiny quick steps rolling heel-to-toe), high-knee marches then high-knee skips, butt-kicks (heels flick up to your bum), straight-leg kicks ("Frankenstein" toe touches), carioca (grapevine, hips twisting side to side), and finish with two smooth build-up strides — start easy and accelerate to about 80% by the end. Take turns leading each one.

Cues: Tall posture, eyes up, quick light contacts with the ground, arms driving from the shoulder (hand from cheek to hip). Think "pop off the ground," not "pound the ground."

🎉 Make it fun: Add robot sound effects on the Frankenstein kicks and a superhero pose at the end of each build-up stride. Whoever has the quietest, springiest feet wins the "ninja runner" title for the round.

A ladder of classic running-form drills down a short lane — high knees, butt-kicks, straight-leg kicks, carioca, build-up strides — jogging back between each. Teaches spring, posture, and quick feet before the run.

Endurance · ~28 min

Adventure Pyramid Run-Walk

Setup: Pick a safe, flat out-and-back stretch — a quiet sidewalk, a park path, or a loop around the block. Put the water bottle at your start/turnaround spot. This is the big one, and it's built gentle: your first run ever, so we keep every jog short and every pace a "talk-and-run" pace.

How it works: Climb the pyramid — jogs get a little longer, then come back down, with a walk break after every jog:

Jog 2 min · walk 1 · jog 2½ · walk 1 · jog 3 (the peak!) · walk 1½ · jog 2½ · walk 1 · jog 2 · walk to finish.
≈ 12 minutes of jogging total. The 3-minute jog at the top is your longest nonstop run — log it below!

Cues: Slow is the goal — you should be able to talk in full sentences the whole jog. Land soft and quiet, relax the shoulders, breathe in a steady rhythm. If a jog feels too hard, ease straight into the walk early — that's smart running, not quitting.

🎉 Make it fun: Name each jog after an adventure — "the volcano climb," "the jungle dash," "the mountain peak." Pick a landmark to jog to (that tree, that mailbox) so each interval has a target. During walk breaks, trade "would-you-rather" questions.

Short jogs that build to a 3-minute peak and ease back down, with a walk break after each — about 12 minutes of easy running total. A gentle, chatty base-builder for a brand-new runner.

Speed & Fun · ~10 min

Sprint Staircase & Chase Races

Setup: Line up three cones at 10, 20, and 30 yards from a start line — that's your "staircase." No ball. Fresh legs make sprints fun, so this comes after a good rest from the run.

How it works: Two parts. First the staircase: run to the 10-yard cone and back (easy jog back), then 20 and back, then 30 and back — building speed each time, resting between. Then chase races: line up side by side for a 20-yard dash and time it (log your best!). Add handicap races where the slower runner gets a head start so it's photo-finish close, and a chase game — leader gets a 2-second head start and tries to reach the far cone before the chaser tags them.

Cues: Explode from the start — lean forward, drive the arms, push the ground back hard for the first few steps. Run through the finish cone, don't slow up early. Full effort, then full rest.

🎉 Make it fun: Dad picks the handicap so the races end in a dive-for-the-line photo finish. Winner of each race calls the next start signal ("ready… set… GO!" or a sneaky "…GORILLA!" fake-out). Log your fastest 20-yard dash below and chase it next running day.

Build-up sprints out to 10/20/30-yard cones, then timed 20-yard dashes and head-start handicap chase races. Pure fast-and-fun speed work to finish on a high.

Cooldown · ~10 min

Walk, Stretch & Chat

Walk it off: Easy walking together until your breathing is fully back to normal and your heart isn't pounding — a good few minutes. Keep sipping water.

Stretch: Give the running muscles some love — calves, quads, hamstrings, hips, and a gentle glute/figure-4 stretch. Hold each about 20 seconds, relaxed, no bouncing.

Chat & recap: While you stretch, each name one thing that felt good about your first run — a jog that flew by, a photo-finish sprint, how the fresh morning felt. Big high-five, then log the session below so it counts as run #1! 🎉

🌧️ Rain Backup (small-space, ~45 min)

Storm rolls in early or the heat's too much? No running room needed — a couple of square feet each is plenty. We swap the run for an in-place cardio + strength circuit that still gets the heart pumping.

Marching & jogging in place (warm-up, 2 rounds of 1 min): High-knee marches, then a light jog in place, then butt-kicks in place — quick light feet, arms driving. Rest 30 seconds between.

In-place cardio intervals (the "run" replacement, 5 rounds): 40 seconds on / 40 seconds easy march off — rotate the "on" move each round: fast feet in place · mountain climbers · star jumps · high-knee run in place · skater hops side-to-side (just a couple feet). This is your engine work for the day.

Strength circuit (2 rounds): 30 seconds each — squats · push-ups (knees OK) · plank hold · glute bridges · little two-foot jumps. Rest a minute between rounds.

Finish: The normal stretch — calves, quads, hamstrings, hips, ~20 seconds each — then trade one "great thing" and a high-five.

🎉 Make it fun: Reaction round with no running room — Dad claps and you switch moves instantly (one clap = fast feet, two claps = freeze in a plank). Miss a call and you owe five star jumps. 😄
