Warm-up · 5 min

Dribble Freeze Dance

Setup: Mark a small square about 10 × 10 yards with four cones. A ball each. Dad is the DJ/caller for the first round.

How it works: Dribble around inside the square with soft little touches — imagine the music is playing. When the caller shouts "FREEZE!" stop the ball dead under your sole and hold a pose. Mix in fun commands: "freeze — foot on top!", "toe taps!", "turn around!", "other foot!". Unfreeze on "dance!" and keep dribbling. Play a couple of minutes, then swap so Son gets to be the caller.

Cues: Keep the ball close — little touches, not big kicks. Head up so you don't bump; eyes flick between the ball and the space. Quick, soft stop on "freeze."

🎉 Make it fun: Silliest freeze pose wins the round. Let Son sneak in a surprise "reverse!" to spin Dad around, and try a slow-motion round where every move is in slow-mo.

Dribble around the square on soft touches; freeze the ball under your foot on the call, then swap callers. Ball control + quick reactions to wake up.

Warm-up · 5 min

Quick-Feet Stick Hops

Setup: Lay 5–6 speed sticks flat on the ground about 1½ feet apart to make a "ladder." No ball for this one — it's a footwork wake-up. Line up at one end.

How it works: Go through the ladder one pattern at a time, then jog back and start the next: two feet in every gap (quick "in-in"), lateral shuffle facing sideways stepping in and out, in-in-out-out, and one-foot hops down the ladder. Take turns leading a pattern for the other to copy. Light and speedy, not big steps.

Cues: Stay on the balls of your feet, knees soft, pump your arms, and look at the sticks only enough to stay clean. Faster feet, not longer steps.

🎉 Make it fun: "Beat the buzzer" — count out loud how many seconds to get through cleanly, then try to shave a little off. Miss a gap and you do a silly wiggle before restarting.

Fast, light footwork through a ladder of flat speed sticks — a few patterns, jog back between each. Pure feet-and-coordination warm-up.

Technical · ~7 min

Ball Mastery Rolls & Taps

Setup: A ball each, a couple of feet of space — no travel needed. This is the "piano scales" of soccer: little touches that make the ball feel like part of your foot.

How it works: Cycle through four moves, ~30 seconds each: toe taps (tap the top of the ball, alternating feet, quick), sole rolls (roll the ball side to side under one foot, then switch), inside-outside taps (tap the ball inside foot to inside foot, keeping it in a small window), and sole roll pull-push (roll it back with the sole, push it forward with laces). Do a round, rest, and go again trying to be a little smoother and faster.

Cues: Small, quick, controlled touches — the ball barely moves. Stay balanced, glance up now and then. Both feet get a turn.

🎉 Make it fun: Count toe taps in 30 seconds and try to beat it (log it below!). Add a "commentator" round where the resting person calls the action like it's the World Cup skills final.

Fast, tiny touches on the spot — toe taps, sole rolls, inside-outside, pull-push. The footwork that makes dribbling feel easy.

Technical · ~8 min

Rebounder Quick-Control Returns

Setup: Stand about 4–5 yards from the rebounding wall, ball at your feet. If you don't have the rebounder handy, any solid flat wall works.

How it works: Pass firmly into the wall, then take a clean first touch to control the ball as it comes back — cushion it and settle it in front of you — then pass again and keep the rhythm going. Count how many clean controls you can string together in 60 seconds. Then run a round left foot only and a round right foot only to grow that weaker side.

Cues: Get your body behind the ball, relax the foot and "cushion" the bounce so it dies right in front of you (a step you can reach), then look up before your next pass. Firm, accurate passes come back cleaner.

🎉 Make it fun: Streak challenge — how many clean touches in a row without a miss? A miss resets the count. Call out "lava floor" — if the ball stops moving you have to restart the streak. Log your best 60-second count below.

Pass into the wall, kill the rebound with a soft first touch, repeat — counting clean controls. The touch that turns a bouncing ball into an easy next play.

Speed & Agility · ~15 min

Rocket T-Drill

Setup: Build a "T" with 4 cones. Put a base cone, then a middle cone 10 yards straight ahead of it, and two top cones 5 yards to the left and 5 yards to the right of the middle one. No ball — this is pure footwork and quick turns.

How it works: From the base, sprint up to the middle cone → shuffle left and touch the left cone → shuffle right all the way across and touch the right cone → shuffle back to the middle → backpedal to the base. That's one run. Time it, catch your breath, and try to beat your time. Give Dad a handicap so the races stay neck-and-neck.

Cues: Stay low and "sit" into your hips on the shuffles, don't cross your feet, touch every cone with your hand, and explode out of each change of direction. Push off the outside foot to turn.

🎉 Make it fun: Photo-finish handicap races — Dad shuffles, Son sprints. Winner invents a victory pose the loser has to copy. Log your fastest T-drill time below and chase it next soccer day.

Sprint up, shuffle left–right touching each cone, then backpedal home — a classic timed change-of-direction race. Great for cutting and stopping fast.

Game · ~15 min

Target Zone Shootout

Setup: Make a goal about 6 feet wide with two cones (or aim at the rebounding wall). Put a small "target" cone just inside each post so there's a hittable corner on each side. Mark a shooting spot about 10–12 yards out. One shooter, one keeper/retriever.

How it works: Each player takes 10 shots. Score 1 point for a goal, and a bonus point for clipping a corner target cone. The keeper tries to make saves. Add up your points out of a possible big score, then swap roles. Best total wins — then run a sudden-death "top-corner only" tiebreaker if you're tied.

Cues: Plant your non-kicking foot beside the ball pointing at the target, strike with the inside of the foot for placement (or laces for power), eyes on the target then down to the ball, and follow through toward where you want it to go.

🎉 Make it fun: Commentate every shot ("he steps up in the 90th minute…!"). Corner-target hits earn a goal celebration of your choice. Let Son name a "worth-double" round. Log your goals out of 10 below and try to beat it.

Ten shots each at a corner-targeted goal — a point per goal, bonus for hitting a corner cone, keeper trying to deny you. Placement, power, and bragging rights.

Cooldown · ~5 min

Walk, Stretch & Recap

Walk it off: Easy walking together, ball tucked under an arm, until your breathing settles. Sip water the whole time.

Stretch: Give the legs some love — calves, quads, hamstrings, hips, and a gentle groin/butterfly stretch. Hold each about 20 seconds, relaxed, no bouncing.

Recap one great thing: Each of you names one awesome thing the other did — a smooth first touch, a top-corner finish, a lightning T-drill turn. Big high-five, then log the session below so it counts toward your streak! 🎉

🌧️ Rain Backup (small-space, ~45 min)

Storm rolls in or the heat's too much? No running room needed — a couple of square feet each is plenty. We keep all the ball feel and add a quick bodyweight circuit.

Standing ball mastery (2 rounds, 30 sec each): toe taps · sole rolls side-to-side · inside-outside taps · sole pull-push — all on the spot, both feet. Count your toe taps in the last 30 seconds and try to beat it.

Wall touches (if you have a safe indoor wall): Gentle cushioned passes and first-touch controls against a wall from a few feet away — soft touches only, keep it controlled indoors. Skip if there's nothing safe to pass against and just do extra sole rolls instead.

Strength & hops circuit (2 rounds): 30 seconds each — squats · push-ups (knees OK) · plank hold · mountain climbers · little two-foot jumps. Rest a minute between rounds.

Finish: The normal stretch — calves, quads, hamstrings, hips, ~20 seconds each — then trade one "great thing" and a high-five.

🎉 Make it fun: Reaction round with no running room — Dad claps and you switch moves instantly (one clap = fast toe taps, two claps = freeze the ball). Miss a call and you owe five squats. 😄
