Warm-up · 5 min

1v1 Ball Tag

Setup: A 10 × 10 yard square marked with four cones. Each of you has a ball.

How it works: Both dribble inside the square at the same time. Try to tap the other person's ball with your own while keeping control of yours. One point per tap. Play 90-second rounds, best of three.

Cues: Head up, small quick touches, turn your body to shield your ball.

🎉 Make it fun: Loser of each round invents a silly celebration dance the winner has to copy!

Both balls live at once — chase the other ball while protecting your own.

Warm-up · 5 min

Red Light, Green Light Dribble

Setup: A start line and a finish line about 15 yards apart, each marked with a cone. Each of you has a ball on the start line.

How it works: Dad is the caller. Green = dribble forward, Red = full stop with your foot on the ball, Yellow = slow-motion dribble. Move on a red light and you take one step back. First ball across the finish wins, then swap the caller.

Cues: Stop the ball dead, freeze balanced, keep your eyes up on the caller.

🎉 Make it fun: Sneak in a "Rainbow!" call — everyone must do a quick roll or step-over trick before dribbling on.

Race to the finish — but only on green. Freeze instantly on red.

Technical · 6 min

Rolls & Taps Ball Mastery

Setup: A small 2-yard box for each person, one ball each. Stand over the ball.

How it works: Move through the circuit twice — 20 toe-taps, 20 sole rolls side-to-side, 10 inside-inside "V" pulls each foot, 10 sole pull-backs. Finish with 30 seconds of freestyle where you pick your favorite moves.

Cues: Light touches on the balls of your feet, ball stays close, glance up between reps.

🎉 Make it fun: "Beat the clock" — count your toe-taps in 30 seconds, then try to break that record on round two.

Rolls and taps keep the ball dancing right under your feet.

Technical · 6 min

Rebounder Wall Give-&-Go

Setup: Stand about 5 yards from the rebounding wall. Place one cone a couple of yards to your side as a "receive gate."

How it works: Pass firmly into the wall, then take a positive first touch out toward the cone — not into your feet. Dribble around the cone and reset. Alternate feet. Count clean two-touch reps; every 4th rep, try a one-touch return straight back to the wall.

Cues: Firm pass, cushion and steer the first touch into space, then accelerate.

🎉 Make it fun: "Combo counter" — how many clean give-and-go's in a row without a miss? Take turns and chase a new family record.

Pass in, first touch out to the cone — then repeat off the other foot.

Speed & Agility · 9 min

Hurdle Shuffle → Speed-Stick Weave → Reaction Sprint

Setup: Three trapezoid hurdles in a row (1 yard apart), then three speed sticks in a line just past them, then two cones — one 5 yards straight ahead and one 5 yards to the right.

How it works: Lateral-shuffle over the three hurdles (quick feet, don't clip them), weave through the speed sticks, then sprint toward the cones. Dad points left or right at the last moment — cut hard to that cone. Jog back to rest. Do 4–6 reps with a short rest between.

Cues: Stay low, pump your arms, plant on the outside foot, and explode out of the cut.

🎉 Make it fun: "Beat Dad" head-start races through the whole course — son gets a fair head start and Dad has to chase.

Quick feet through the gear, then react to Dad's call and cut hard.

Game · 9 min

Champions 1v1 + Shooting Points

Setup: Two mini-goals made of cones about 12 yards apart. Balls stacked to the side for the shooting round.

How it works: Part A (5 min): 1v1 — the attacker tries to beat the defender and score in the little goal; switch roles each turn and keep score. Part B (4 min): shooting challenge — place balls at growing distances and earn points for hitting the corners, using the rebounding wall or Dad as keeper.

Cues: Change your pace to beat the defender, shoot low into the corners, and always follow your shot.

🎉 Make it fun: Commentary mode — Dad does a World Cup announcer voice for every goal, and the winner gets a big final-whistle celebration.

Beat the defender with a change of pace, then finish into the corner.

Cooldown · 5 min

Walk, Stretch & Recap

Walk it off: Easy walk together for 2–3 minutes until your breathing settles.

Stretch: Calves, quads, hamstrings, and hip flexors — hold each about 20 seconds, nice and relaxed.

Recap: Each of you names one thing the other did great today. Finish with a high-five.

🌧️ Rain Backup (small-space, ~30–40 min)

Storming or soaked? Run this indoors in a living-room-sized space — no running room needed, just a couple of square feet each and one ball.

Circuit (3 rounds, side by side): 45 seconds of work, 15 seconds rest, straight through — squats · push-ups (knees OK) · mountain climbers · plank hold · jumping jacks · ball toe taps · ball sole rolls. Rest a minute between rounds. Tight on ceiling? Swap the jumping jacks for stepping up and down a stair.

Mirror game (3 rounds): One of you leads in-place moves — hops, arm waves, freezes, silly poses — and the other mirrors as fast as they can. Switch leader each round.

Finish: The normal stretch — calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, ~20 seconds each — then recap one great thing about each other and a high-five.

🎉 Make it fun: "Coach's call" — take turns shouting the next move like a drill sergeant with a goofy voice. Loser of the mirror game picks the celebration dance.
