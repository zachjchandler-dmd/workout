Warm-up · 5 min

1. Dribble Tag (1v1 ball tag)

Setup: Mark a small square about 12 × 12 yards with four cones. Each of you has a ball.

How it works: Both dribble inside the square at the same time. The goal is to gently tap the other person's ball with your foot while keeping your own under control. Every tap = 1 point. Play three 60-second rounds, checking the score between rounds.

Cues:

  • Little touches, ball close to your feet.
  • Head up so you can hunt and protect.
  • Turn away and shield when Dad gets close.
🎉 Make it fun: Loser of each round picks the next "silly dribble" — hop-scotch dribble, tiptoe dribble, or dribble like a crab. Dad plays it up big.

Keep your ball close while chasing Dad's ball inside the square.

Warm-up · 5 min

2. Coach Says — Form Drill Freeze

Setup: Line up two cones about 15 yards apart. No ball needed. This wakes up running muscles and adds a stop/start reaction game.

How it works: One person is the "coach" and calls a running-form move to do cone-to-cone: high knees, butt kicks, skips, side shuffles, giant lunges. But it only counts if the coach says "Coach says" first — if they just shout the move, you must FREEZE. Swap who's coach halfway.

Cues:

  • High knees: tall posture, quick light feet.
  • Butt kicks: heels tap your seat, stay springy.
  • Freeze fast and balanced — that's the reaction part.
🎉 Make it fun: When someone gets faked out and moves on a no-"Coach says", they do 3 goofy star jumps. Dad should get faked out at least once on purpose.

Move cone-to-cone only on "Coach says" — otherwise freeze!

Main run · 25 min

3. Checkpoint Adventure Run/Walk

Setup: Pick a flat loop or path (park, sidewalk, or field perimeter). Use cones or real landmarks (a tree, a bench, a lamppost) as "checkpoints." This is session #1 of running — we're building a base gently.

How it works: Alternate easy jogging and walking. Before each jog, your son picks the next checkpoint to reach:

  • Easy warm jog to loosen up: 2 min.
  • Then 6 rounds of: jog 2 min → walk 1 min.
  • That's about 12 minutes of running total — perfect for a first base-builder.

Cues:

  • "Conversation pace" — if he can't chat, slow down.
  • Relaxed shoulders, easy arms, breathe steady.
  • Effort by feel, not speed. Sip water on the walk breaks.
🎉 Make it fun: He's the "expedition captain" and names each checkpoint ("the dragon tree," "base camp"). Give the whole loop a story — you're explorers reaching new territory each jog.

Son picks the next checkpoint before each jog interval.

Fun speed · 10 min

4. Countdown Rockets & Animal Races

Setup: Two cones ~20 yards apart as a start and finish line. Lay a speed stick flat 5 yards past the start as a "launch marker" (optional).

How it works: Two quick games, short and playful — these are strides, not all-out max sprints:

  • Countdown Rockets (×4): Both crouch at the line. Count "3–2–1–blast off!" and accelerate smoothly to the far cone at about 80%. Walk back to recover fully.
  • Animal Races (×3): Race the 20 yards a fun way — bear crawl, frog jumps, then a real sprint for the finale. Give son a head start so it's close.

Cues:

  • Drive the arms, quick feet out of the start.
  • Smooth and tall — not straining.
  • Full walk-back rest between reps so each one feels fast.
🎉 Make it fun: Dad does the animal moves too (slow and dramatic!) and lets the last sprint come down to a photo-finish lean at the line.

Smooth accelerations to the far cones — full walk-back rest between reps.

Cooldown · 10 min

Walk it out & stretch

Easy 3–4 minute walk together until breathing settles. Then gentle stretches, holding each about 15–20 seconds:

  • Calf stretch against a fence or tree.
  • Quad stretch (hold one foot behind you).
  • Reach for toes, then reach for the sky.
  • Big shoulder rolls and shake-outs.
💬 Recap: Tell your son one specific thing he did great today — "your form freezes were lightning fast" or "you kept a steady jog the whole way." Ask him his favorite part.
