Warm-up · 5 min

Traffic Lights Dribbling

Setup: A loose square about 12–15 yards across, marked with four cones. Each of you has a ball at your feet inside the square.

How it works: Dribble around the square keeping the ball close. Dad calls out colors: "Green" = dribble at a good pace, "Yellow" = slow, tiny controlled touches, "Red" = stop the ball dead with your sole. Mix in "Reverse!" (turn and go the other way) and "Speed bump!" (a quick toe-tap on top of the ball). Swap who's the caller halfway.

Cues: Head up so you can hear and see the call, soft little touches, keep the ball inside the square, stop it cleanly on red.

🎉 Make it fun: Add a silly bonus color — "Purple = spin move!" or "Rainbow = do a dance." Whoever reacts slowest on a red does a goofy victory wiggle.

Both dribble inside the square, reacting instantly to green / yellow / red calls.

Warm-up · 5 min

Body-Part Ball Taps

Setup: Stand a few feet apart, each with a ball resting on the ground in front of you. No space needed — this is a reaction and quick-touch game.

How it works: Dad calls a body part — "Right foot!" "Left foot!" "Knee!" "Elbow!" "Both hands!" "Sit on it!" — and you race to touch the ball with that part, then pop back up ready. Speed the calls up as you warm up. After a minute, son becomes the caller. Sprinkle in a few "Toe taps — go!" (10 quick taps, alternating feet on top of the ball).

Cues: Stay on your toes, bend the knees, react fast, and get back to a ready position every time.

🎉 Make it fun: Throw in a fake-out — call "El-… bow- tie!" and see who twitches. Nobody's allowed to move until the whole word is out. Giggles guaranteed.

React to each call, touch the ball with that body part, and bounce back to ready.

Technical · ~8 min

Rebounder One-Touch Passing

Setup: Stand about 3–4 yards from the rebounder wall, ball at your feet. A cone marks your spot so you don't drift in.

How it works: Pass the ball into the wall and try to return it with a single clean touch — pass, receive, pass — keeping a rhythm going ("give-and-go" with the wall). Count how many one-touch passes you string together before it gets away. Do a few rounds each: start with your strong foot, then challenge yourself with your weak foot.

Cues: Use the inside of the foot like a putter, firm but soft, ankle locked. Watch the ball onto your foot, and stay light so you can adjust to the rebound.

🎉 Make it fun: Play "don't break the chain" — every clean one-touch pass adds a link, but if the ball stops you start a new chain. Try to beat your best chain of the morning.

Pass into the wall and return it in one touch — count your longest unbroken chain.

Ball Skills · ~7 min

Figure-8 Foundation Dribble

Setup: Two cones about 3 yards apart. That's your whole course.

How it works: Dribble the ball in a figure-8 around the two cones, weaving through the middle each lap. Use inside foot to curl around one cone and outside foot to curl around the other, keeping the ball glued close. Go smooth for a couple laps, then time a 60-second round and count full figure-8 laps.

Cues: Small quick touches, ball never more than a step away, knees bent, eyes flicking up between touches. Smooth beats fast — control first.

🎉 Make it fun: Race the clock: how many clean laps in 60 seconds? Then let son try to beat Dad's number — winner picks the music for cooldown.

Curl around each cone and weave the middle — inside foot one way, outside foot the other.

Speed & Agility · ~8 min

5-Cone Star Drill

Setup: One cone in the middle and four cones around it like the points of a star, each about 4 yards out (front, back, left, right). No ball for this one — pure footwork and change of direction.

How it works: Start at the middle cone. Sprint out to an outside cone, touch it, backpedal or shuffle back to the middle, then explode out to the next one — work your way around all four points. That's one "star lap." Time it. Rest a bit, then try to beat your time. Take turns and cheer each other on.

Cues: Push off hard, stay low on the turns, plant your outside foot to change direction, and pump your arms. Quick and controlled — don't overrun the cone.

🎉 Make it fun: Dad calls the cones in a random order ("Left! Back! Right!") so son has to react instead of memorize. Then swap — son bosses Dad around the star.

Explode from the middle to each point and back — one lap around the star, against the clock.

Game · ~8 min

Sharpshooter Cone Knockdown

Setup: Stand up 3–4 cones as "targets" against or in front of the rebounder wall (or balance a cone on top of a bigger one for a tower). Mark a shooting line about 7–8 yards back with a speed stick or cone.

How it works: Take turns shooting from the line, trying to knock down a target. 1 point for hitting a target, 2 points for knocking the tower clean off. Play to 7, or count how many targets you hit in a round of 5 shots. Move the line back a step whenever it's getting too easy.

Cues: Plant your non-kicking foot beside the ball, strike with your laces (or inside foot for accuracy), eyes on the target, and follow through toward it.

🎉 Make it fun: Name the targets ("that one's the dragon!") and do a commentary voice for each shot. Buzzer-beater rule: last shot of the game is worth double.

Shoot from the line to knock down the cone targets — 1 point a hit, 2 for the tower.

Cooldown · ~4 min

Walk, Stretch & Recap

Walk it off: Easy lap or two around the yard together until your breathing settles.

Stretch: Calves, quads, hamstrings, and hips — hold each about 20 seconds, nice and gentle.

Recap one great thing: Each of you names one thing the other did really well today — a slick touch, a great shot, a fast star lap. Finish with a high-five.
