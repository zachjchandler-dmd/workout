Warm-up · 0–5 min

Passing Gates

Setup: Scatter three "gates" (2 cones ~1 yard apart) around a small ~10×10 yard area. One ball between you.

How it works: Move around together and pass the ball to each other through any gate. After a completed pass through a gate, jog to a different gate. Count how many clean gate-passes you can make in 60 seconds, then try to beat it.

  • Pass with the inside of the foot — ankle locked, toe up.
  • Call your partner's name before you pass.
  • Light, bouncy feet — always moving to a new gate.
Make it fun: Every time you break your own record, both of you do a goal celebration of the son's choice. 🎉
Warm-up · 5–10 min

Traffic Light Dribble

Setup: Mark a ~8×8 yard box with 4 cones. Each of you has a ball inside the box.

How it works: Dribble around inside the box. Dad calls the light: Green = dribble at speed, Yellow = tiny slow touches, Red = stop the ball dead under your sole. Switch who's the caller halfway.

  • On "Red," freeze instantly — sole on top of the ball, still and balanced.
  • Keep the ball close — little touches, eyes up to hear the call.
  • Use both feet as you turn around the box.
Make it fun: Add a surprise call — "Purple = spin move!" Whoever reacts last does 3 toe-taps. Let the son invent a color.
Technical · 10–17 min

Ball Mastery Foundations

Setup: Each with a ball, standing a few feet apart so you can watch and copy each other. No cones needed.

How it works: Work through three moves, ~40 seconds each, twice around: (1) Sole rolls — roll the ball side to side with the bottom of the foot; (2) Toe taps — light taps on top of the ball, alternating feet; (3) Foundations (bell touches) — tap the ball back and forth between the insides of both feet.

  • Stay on the balls of your feet, knees soft.
  • Small, quick, controlled touches — quality over speed.
  • Head up for a peek between touches.
Make it fun: "Copycat" — son leads a move and dad has to mirror it exactly. Then swap the leader. Silliest move allowed once. 😄
Technical · 17–25 min

Rebounder Wall One-Touch

Setup: Stand ~5 yards from the rebounder wall, one ball. Place a cone where you'll receive so you learn to control into space.

How it works: Pass into the wall, then control the return with your first touch toward the cone and pass again. Do 10 with the right foot, 10 with the left, then 10 alternating. Dad and son take turns — the resting partner counts clean touches.

  • First touch out of your feet, into space toward the cone — not stuck under you.
  • Cushion the ball: relax the foot as it arrives.
  • Firm, accurate pass back into the wall.
Make it fun: "Streak game" — how many clean pass-and-controls in a row before a miss? Beat your partner's best streak to win a point. 🏆
Speed & Agility · 25–40 min

Weave–Hurdle–Sprint Course

Setup: A line of 4 speed sticks ~1 yard apart, then 2 trapezoid hurdles ~1.5 yards apart, then a finish cone ~8 yards past the hurdles. Total ~15 yards.

How it works: (1) Quick side-to-side weave through the speed sticks; (2) fast feet over the hurdles (one step between); (3) explode in a straight sprint to the finish cone. Walk back to recover. Do 5–6 runs. First 2 without a ball, then dribble a ball through the weave on the later runs.

  • Pump the arms — they drive the legs.
  • Short choppy steps in the weave, big powerful steps in the sprint.
  • Accelerate through the finish cone, don't slow early.
Make it fun: Dad races too, but starts 2 seconds later (a "head-start handicap") — can the son hold the lead to the cone? Best of 5. 🏁
Game · 40–55 min

1v1 to Mini-Goals + Shooting Points

Setup: Two small cone goals (2 cones each, ~2 yards wide) about 12 yards apart. One ball.

How it works: Play 1v1 — win the ball, dribble past your parent, and score in their goal. First to 5 goals. Then finish with a shooting challenge: 5 shots each from the cone — 1 point on target, 2 points if it goes between the cones without being saved. Highest total wins.

  • Attack with pace, then a change of direction to beat the defender.
  • Defending: stay low, patient, don't dive in.
  • Shooting: plant your non-kicking foot beside the ball, strike with the laces.
Make it fun: Dad plays defense on his knees for the 1v1 to keep it fair and giggly. Loser of each round does a silly dance the winner picks. 🕺
Cooldown · 55–60 min

Walk, Stretch & Recap

How it works: Slow walk together for a minute to let the heart rate come down, then easy stretches: quad pull, calf stretch, hamstring reach, and big arm circles — hold each ~15 seconds.

  • Breathe slow and deep; no bouncing in the stretches.
  • Sip water.
Recap moment: Each of you names one thing the other did great today. End with a high-five and pick something to look forward to on Wednesday. 🙌
