Warm-up · 5 min

Musical Cones Dribble

Setup: Scatter 6–8 cones around a 12×12-yard box. Each of you has a ball inside the box — it's a soccer day, so the ball's in from the very first minute. Have one fewer cone than players once you're racing for them (with just the two of you, take one cone away so there's a scramble).

How it works: Both dribble around the box, keeping the ball close and heads up. When Dad shouts "Freeze!" you both race to stop your ball dead on top of the nearest cone with your sole. Last one to a cone (or whoever misses) does a quick fun forfeit — three toe taps — then straight back in. Play 60–90 second rounds and let Son be the caller for a round too.

Cues: Little touches with the laces and inside of the foot, eyes up to spot the open cones, and settle the ball under your sole nice and controlled — no stamping it away.

🎉 Make it fun: Dad calls a colour or a number instead of "Freeze!" and you have to park your ball on that cone. Get sneaky — call two in a row! 😄

A dribble-and-react game where a shout sends you racing to trap your ball on a cone. Sneaks in tons of close control, head-up awareness and a crisp sole-stop while it just feels like a game.

Warm-up · 5 min

Follow-the-Leader Warm-Up Flow

Setup: No ball for this one. Mark a lane about 12–15 yards with a cone at each end. This is a movement warm-up to get the hips, legs and back loose and springy before the ball skills.

How it works: One person leads down the lane doing a dynamic move and the other copies right behind, then you walk back and swap the leader. Cycle through: walking knee hugs → forward lunge with a reach-up → walking leg swings → side shuffle → skips for height → heel-to-butt jog → straight-leg "toy soldier" kicks. One length each, about 5 minutes.

Cues: Move smooth and controlled, reach through the full range, stand tall between moves. This is waking the body up — it's not a race.

🎉 Make it fun: The leader gets to add one silly "signature move" of their own each round and the follower has to nail it exactly. Best copycat of the day earns bragging rights! 🕺

A no-ball follow-the-leader dynamic warm-up — knee hugs, lunges, leg swings, shuffles, skips and toy soldiers, one length at a time. Loosens hips and hamstrings and fires up the body before the ball work.

Technical · ~7 min

Roll-&-Chop Gates

Setup: Set 4–5 little 1-yard cone "gates" scattered a few yards apart. One ball. Today's combo move is the roll-and-chop: roll the ball sideways across your body with the sole of one foot, then chop it back the other way with the inside of the other foot — a quick shimmy that leaves a defender going the wrong way.

How it works: Dribble to a gate, do one clean roll-and-chop right at the gate to change direction, then attack the next gate. Count how many gates you get through with a clean roll-and-chop in 60 seconds — that's your number to log. Then Son and Dad switch. Do 2–3 timed rounds, resetting between.

Cues: Sole rolls the ball softly across (don't stamp), then the inside of the other foot snaps it back and you explode out. Sell it with a little hip and shoulder fake, and keep the touches small.

🎉 Make it fun: Give each gate a defender's name to "beat." A clean roll-and-chop is a point; one where the ball gets away costs you a "do-over" gate. Beat your own count each round! ⚡

Dribble gate to gate doing a clean roll-then-chop at each one to wrong-foot a defender, counting clean gates in 60 seconds. Builds sole control, deceptive direction changes and confident close dribbling.

Technical · ~8 min

Rebounder Pass-and-Move

Setup: Stand 3–4 yards from the rebounder wall with one ball. Put a cone a few steps to your left and another to your right. This drill trains you to pass, move, and then control the ball on the go — like a real game, where you never receive standing still.

How it works: Pass the ball firmly into the wall, then immediately shuffle to one of the side cones, and take a clean first touch on the rebound as you arrive — settling it under control. Pass again, shuffle to the other cone, control again. Count how many clean moving first touches you string together in 60 seconds (that's your number to log). Try a round each foot.

Cues: Push off and get your feet moving the instant you pass, then cushion the first touch — "soft foot," like catching an egg — into the space you're heading. Stay light on your toes and always be arriving as the ball does.

🎉 Make it fun: Dad points left or right the moment you pass, and you have to move to that cone — no cheating early! Nail 5 in a row and Dad owes you his silliest goal celebration. 📣

Pass into the wall, shuffle to a side cone, and take a clean first touch on the move — counting your clean moving touches in 60 seconds. Sharpens first touch, footwork and receiving in space like a real game.

Speed & Agility · ~15 min

Triangle Turbo Circuit

Setup: No ball — pure footwork and speed. Build the circuit in order: (1) speed-stick ladder flat on the ground → (2) 3 trapezoid hurdles to hop over → (3) a triangle of 3 cones about 5 yards apart → (4) a 12-yard sprint through a finish cone. Phone/watch to time it.

How it works: Go through it as one flowing run — quick feet down the ladder, snappy two-foot hops over the hurdles, then work the triangle: sprint to cone 1, side-shuffle to cone 2, backpedal to cone 3 — then explode out on the finish sprint. Dad times the whole thing; that's your number to log. Rest fully (about a minute) and go again 3–4 times, chasing a faster lap.

Cues: Fast light feet in the ladder (quality first), land soft and springy off the hurdles, stay low with quick choppy steps around the triangle, and run through the finish — don't slow up early.

🎉 Make it fun: It's a "beat the clock" grand prix — Son and Dad each get a lap, and Dad runs the triangle the opposite way round as a handicap. Announce the leaderboard after every run. 🏎️

A four-station speed circuit — ladder, hurdles, a sprint-shuffle-backpedal triangle, then a sprint — timed end to end. Builds quick feet, spring, sharp multi-direction movement and acceleration, with a time to beat each round.

Game · ~15 min

End Line 1v1 Showdown

Setup: Mark a channel about 15 yards long and 10 wide with cones — one "end line" for each of you at opposite ends. One ball. This is a proper 1v1 duel: you score by dribbling the ball under control over your opponent's end line.

How it works: Start face-to-face in the middle, ball between you. Whoever wins it attacks their target end line while the other defends. Get the ball stopped under your sole on or past the line and that's a point. If the ball goes out or the defender wins it clean, reset in the middle. First to 5 wins the match; play a couple of matches and log your rounds won.

Cues: Attacking — use the moves from today (roll-and-chop!), shift the defender's weight with a fake, then burst past into space. Defending — stay low, patient, side-on, and jab in to nick the ball only when it's a touch too far from their foot.

🎉 Make it fun: Give each match a "golden goal" bonus — a point scored with a proper skill move (a roll-and-chop or a clean beat) counts double. Dad defends with hands behind his back to keep it fair. 🥇

A 1v1 duel where you score by dribbling under control over your opponent's end line — attack, defend, reset, first to 5. Puts every skill together: dribbling moves, shielding, and reading the defender.

Cooldown · ~5 min

Walk, Stretch & Recap

Walk it off: Easy 2–3 minute walk together, gently dribbling the ball at a stroll, until your breathing settles. Keep sipping water — it's heading for a hot one.

Stretch: Give the legs some love — calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, and glutes. Hold each about 20 seconds, relaxed, no bouncing.

🌧️ Rain Backup (small-space, ~60 min)

Storm rolls in early or you're stuck inside? No field or running room needed — a couple of square feet each is plenty. We keep the ball touches going with in-place foot skills and add a bodyweight circuit so it still counts as your soccer-day workout.

Warm-up (2 rounds of 90 sec): March in place → in-place toe taps on top of the ball → jumping jacks, light and quick. Rest 30 seconds between rounds.

Ball mastery (6 rounds, 40 sec on / 20 sec off), all on the spot: toe taps · sole rolls side-to-side · inside-inside taps (bell) · pull-push (sole back, laces forward) · foundations (inside-foot taps) · a roll-and-chop shimmy in place. Count your best 30 seconds of toe taps if you want a number for the record book.

Strength circuit (2 rounds): 30 seconds each — squats · push-ups (knees OK) · plank hold · glute bridges · calf raises. Rest a minute between rounds.

Finish: The normal stretch — calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, glutes, ~20 seconds each — then trade one "proud moment" and a high-five.

🎉 Make it fun: Reaction round — Dad claps and you switch skills instantly (one clap = toe taps, two claps = freeze with your foot on the ball). Miss a call and you owe five squats. 😄
