Warm-up · 5 min

Number Cone Scramble

Setup: No ball. Spread 5 cones in a fan about 8–10 yards out and number them 1 to 5 (just remember which is which). Son stands on a start cone in the middle, ready like a sprinter.

How it works: Dad calls a number — Son reacts instantly, sprints to that cone, taps it, and jogs back to start for the next call. Mix it up: call two numbers in a row, or add a style — "backpedal to 3," "side-shuffle to 5." Keep it quick and light for about 5 minutes to wake up the legs and the reactions.

Cues: Explode on the word — first three steps low and punchy, arms driving. Ease off and stay balanced as you reach the cone so you don't overrun it.

🎉 Make it fun: Dad throws in fake-outs — "threeeee… TWO!" — and you score a point for every clean, no-flinch start. Then swap: you call the numbers and Dad has to scramble. 🎯

React to Dad's called number, sprint to that cone, jog back. A no-ball warm-up that fires up reactions, first-step quickness, and acceleration while the legs get warm.

Warm-up · 5 min

Olympic Form Ladder

Setup: No ball. Mark a lane about 15 yards long with a cone at each end. This is a running-form warm-up that teaches your body to run tall, quick, and springy.

How it works: Do one form drill down the lane, then walk back and start the next — climb the "ladder" of drills: A-marches → A-skips → B-skips → high-knee run → butt-kicks → power skips (for height) → straight-leg bounds → build-up stride (start easy, finish fast). One length each; about 5 minutes total.

Cues: Stand tall like a puppet on a string, quick light feet ("kiss the ground"), knees driving forward, arms swinging cheek-to-cheek. Smooth and springy, not a max effort.

🎉 Make it fun: Strike an "Olympic podium pose" at the end of every length and Dad awards style points out of 10. Nail the build-up stride and you get the gold-medal ceremony. 🥇

Climb a ladder of running-form drills — marches, skips, high knees, butt-kicks, bounds and a build-up stride — one length at a time. Grooves tall posture, quick feet, and springy strides before the run.

Endurance · ~28 min

Level-Up Ladder Run

Setup: Pick a safe flat stretch — a quiet sidewalk, a park loop, or two cones about 40–50 yards apart to jog between. Phone/watch to time the segments. This is your very first run session, so we keep it gentle and build the engine one level at a time.

How it works: Five jog "levels" that climb then hold, each followed by a 1-minute walk to recover: Level 1 = 1.5 min jog · Level 2 = 2 min · Level 3 = 2.5 min · Level 4 = 3 min · Level 5 = 3 min. That's about 12 minutes of running in total, all at an easy pace. The whole time, keep it slow enough that you could chat — that's the golden rule today.

Cues: Easy, conversational "talk-test" pace — if you can't talk, slow down. Relaxed shoulders, short quick steps, breathe smooth and steady. Walk breaks are part of the plan, not a fail.

🎉 Make it fun: You "unlock" a new level with every jog — call them out like a video game ("Level 3… UNLOCKED!"). Run side by side with Dad and keep a story going the whole way to prove you're at talking pace. 🎮

Five climbing jog "levels" (1.5→2→2.5→3→3 min) with a 1-minute walk between each — about 12 minutes of easy, conversational running. A gentle base-builder for a first-time runner, done shoulder-to-shoulder with Dad.

Speed · ~10 min

Turbo Ladder & Pursuit Races

Setup: Mark a start line, then cones at 10, 15, and 20 yards straight ahead. Phone/watch for one timed dash. Fully rested legs before each sprint — this is short and fast, not tiring.

How it works: First the turbo ladder — 2 build-up accelerations to the 10-yd cone, 2 to the 15, 2 to the 20, walking back to recover each time. Then one all-out timed 20-yard dash (Dad times it — that's today's record). Finish with pursuit races: Son gets a head start, Dad chases from a few yards back, race to a finish cone. Son picks how big Dad's handicap is.

Cues: Powerful arm drive, lean forward on the first steps and push the ground back, then rise up tall as you hit top speed. Run through the finish line — never slow down early.

🎉 Make it fun: You're the "handicapper" — set Dad's head-start so the races are photo-finish close. Winner does a slow-motion victory replay across the line. 🏁

A short acceleration ladder to 10/15/20 yards, one timed 20-yard dash, then handicap pursuit races against Dad. Builds explosive first steps and top-end speed — with a dash time to chase.

Cooldown · ~10 min

Walk, Stretch & Chat

Walk it off: Easy 3–4 minute walk together until your breathing settles and your legs feel calm. Keep sipping water — it's heading for a hot one.

Stretch: Give the running muscles some love — calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, and glutes. Hold each about 20 seconds, relaxed, no bouncing.

Chat: While you stretch, each name one thing you're proud of from today — a level you unlocked, your best dash, a photo-finish race, or just that you got run #1 done. Big high-five, then tap a button below so it counts. Loved a drill? Tap its ⭐! 🎉

🌧️ Rain Backup (small-space, ~60 min)

Storm rolls in early or the heat's just too much? No field or running room needed — a couple of square feet each is plenty. We keep the running engine working with in-place cardio and add a bodyweight circuit, so it still counts as your run-day workout.

Warm-up (2 rounds of 90 sec): March in place → jumping jacks → in-place fast feet, light on your toes. Rest 30 seconds between rounds.

Cardio intervals (8 rounds, 40 sec on / 30 sec off): Rotate — high-knees · butt-kicks · fast feet · mountain climbers · skater hops side to side · quick step-ups on a low stair · squat jumps · in-place shuffle. All in place — this is the "run" for a rainy day. Count your best 30 seconds of high-knees if you want a number for the record book.

Strength circuit (2 rounds): 30 seconds each — squats · push-ups (knees OK) · plank hold · glute bridges · calf raises. Rest a minute between rounds.

Finish: The normal stretch — calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, glutes, ~20 seconds each — then trade one "proud moment" and a high-five.

🎉 Make it fun: Reaction round — Dad claps and you switch moves instantly (one clap = fast feet, two claps = freeze in a plank). Miss a call and you owe five squats. 😄
