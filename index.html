Warm-up · 5 min

Pac-Man Dribble Chase

Setup: Scatter 8–10 cones around a ~15×15-yard area as the "dots." Son has a ball (this is a soccer day, so ball work is welcome in the warm-up). Dad is the "ghost" — no ball, just chasing.

How it works: Son dribbles around gobbling up dots — steer the ball right past each cone to "eat" it — while the ghost (Dad) tries to tag him. If Dad gets close, Son reacts and changes direction sharply to escape. Get tagged? Do 3 quick toe-taps to respawn and keep going. Play ~2 minutes, then swap roles so Dad dribbles and Son is the ghost. Head up the whole time.

Cues: Small, controlled touches close to the feet, eyes up to spot the ghost and the next dot, and cut away hard the instant Dad closes in.

🎉 Make it fun: Full arcade mode — "waka-waka" sounds while you eat dots, and a spooky ghost noise from whoever's chasing. Knock over a special "power cone" and the ghost has to freeze and count to three! 👻⚽

A dribble-chase warm-up: collect the cone "dots" while dodging the ghost. Gets the whole body moving and drills close control, scanning, and quick changes of direction — all with a grin.

Warm-up · 5 min

Mirror Mobility Flow

Setup: No ball. Stand side by side in a ~15-yard lane with a cone at each end. This is a dynamic mobility flow done "mirror" style — one leads a move, the other copies — so every joint gets warm before the skill work.

How it works: The leader picks a move and you both do it down the lane, then walk back and switch leader for the next one: leg swings (front-back & side-to-side) → walking lunges with an overhead reach → high-knee march → butt-kicks → carioca (grapevine) → A-skips → straight-leg kicks (toy soldiers). Trade the leader role each trip. About 5 minutes total.

Cues: Big, controlled range of motion — reach tall, swing loose, no bouncing or forcing. Warm and springy is the goal, not tired.

🎉 Make it fun: The follower has to copy the leader exactly — including one silly flourish the leader sneaks in (a spin, a superhero pose). Botch the mirror and you owe the other person a goofy dance. 🪞😄

A no-ball dynamic mobility flow done as a mirror game — swings, lunges, high knees, carioca, skips, and kicks with the lead swapping each trip. Warms hips, hamstrings, and shoulders and sneaks in reaction and focus.

Technical · ~8 min

Outside-Foot Cut Gates

Setup: Scatter 4–5 little cone gates (about 2 feet wide) around your area. You'll need your ball. This drill grooves the outside-of-the-foot touch — the sharp, sneaky cut great dribblers use to change direction and dart into space.

How it works: Dribble to a gate and, as you go through, push the ball with the outside of your foot to cut sharply toward the next gate — no inside-foot, no sole, just the outside. Then dribble on and cut through the next one. Count how many clean outside-foot cut gates you can do in 60 seconds — Dad works the timer. Use both feet across the run.

Cues: Little toe-side taps, not big swings; stay low and balanced; and cut with the same foot's outside then power away in the new direction. A rep counts only if the touch through the gate is genuinely with the outside of the foot.

🎉 Make it fun: Every clean outside-foot cut is you "swerving past" an invisible defender — shout their name as you leave them behind. Dad tries it too and you vote on whose cut is sharpest. 💨⚽

Dribble each gate and change direction with the outside of the foot. Builds a quick, disguised cutting touch, close control, and both-footed confidence — counted against the clock.

Technical · ~8 min

Rebounder Rapid-Fire Passing

Setup: Stand ~4–5 yards from the rebounding wall (any flat wall works). You'll need your ball. This one trains a crisp one-touch pass and a lively, reactive body.

How it works: Pass the ball into the wall and, when it comes back, pass it straight back first-time — one touch — keeping a smooth rhythm going. Start easy and short; as you find the groove, speed it up and try to alternate feet. Count how many clean one-touch passes you can string together in 60 seconds (each controlled first-time pass = 1). Dad works the timer.

Cues: Get square to the wall, use the inside of the foot like a putter, and meet the ball with a firm, still ankle. Stay on the balls of your feet and give a little to the ball so it doesn't bounce away.

🎉 Make it fun: Play "don't break the chain" — count out loud together and see how high you can climb before a miss resets it. Every 10 clean in a row earns a celebration shout. Then Dad takes a turn and you try to beat his streak. 🔥⚽

Quick-fire one-touch passing off the wall. Sharpens passing weight, a reactive first touch, and both-footed rhythm — with a clean-passes-in-a-minute number to chase.

Speed & Agility · ~15 min

Rocket Ladder Blitz Circuit

Setup: Build one line of stations: speed-stick ladder → two trapezoid hurdles → a 4-cone agility box → a 12-yard sprint finish. No ball for this one — it's pure footwork and speed.

How it works: Run the whole thing as one timed lap: two quick feet in each ladder rung, two-footed hops over the trapezoids, then work the 4-cone box — sprint up one side, shuffle across, backpedal down, shuffle back — and finally explode into the 12-yard sprint through the finish. Walk all the way back to rest, then go again. Log your fastest clean lap — a lap counts only if you hit every rung, hop, and cone.

Cues: Quick, light feet in the ladder; land soft and spring straight back up over the hurdles; stay low with choppy steps on the box shuffles and backpedal; then run tall and pump the arms on the sprint. Full rest between reps — speed needs fresh legs.

🎉 Make it fun: Dad runs the blitz too — best clean lap wins, loser does a slow-mo replay of the winner's finish-line celebration. Count down "3-2-1-BLITZ!" before each go and add a rocket "whoosh" on the sprint. 🚀⚡

A four-station agility blitz run against the clock — ladder, hurdles, an agility box, and a sprint. Trains footwork, change of direction, and acceleration, with a fastest-clean-lap record to chase.

Game · ~15 min

Steal-the-Ball Duel

Setup: Make two small cone goals facing each other about 12–15 yards apart. Place a ball on the ground halfway between them. Son stands by one goal, Dad by the other. Have a few spare balls handy if you can.

How it works: Dad shouts "GO!" and you both sprint to the middle ball. Whoever wins it becomes the attacker and tries to score in the other person's goal; the other defends. A goal = 1 point, then reset with a fresh ball in the middle. Play 10 rounds and count how many you win. To keep it fair and fun, Dad starts a step or two farther back and plays "gentle defender."

Cues: Explode off the "GO," get your body to the ball first and shield it, then attack quickly with your head up. On defense, stay low and patient — don't dive in; jockey and time your poke.

🎉 Make it fun: Mix up the start — Dad calls colors and you only go on "green," or you both start sitting down or facing away. Big-match commentary on every goal, and the round winner gets to pick the next silly starting position. 🥅⚡

A reaction-start 1v1 to two goals — race for the ball, then attack or defend. Rolls the day's touches, cutting, and shielding into a real duel with a score on the board and plenty to celebrate.

Cooldown · ~5 min

Walk, Stretch & Recap

Walk it off: Easy 2–3 minute walk together until your breathing settles. Keep sipping water — it warms up quickly after that cool start.

Stretch: Give the legs some love — calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, and groin (great after all those cuts and sprints). Hold each about 20 seconds, relaxed, no bouncing.

Recap: As you stretch, each name one great thing from today — a sharp outside-foot cut, a long passing chain, a duel you stole. Big high-five, then tap a button below so it counts. Loved a drill? Tap its ⭐! 🎉

🌧️ Rain Backup (small-space, ~60 min)

Fog turns to a downpour or you're stuck inside? No running room needed — a couple of square feet each is plenty. We keep the ball skills going in place and add a bodyweight circuit for the engine.

Warm-up (2 rounds of 90 sec): march in place → arm circles & trunk twists → light in-place fast feet. Rest 30 seconds between rounds.

In-place ball mastery (3 rounds of 40 sec on / 20 off): toe taps on top of the ball → side-to-side sole rolls → foundations (inside-foot taps) → sole pull-push. Tiny space, big touches — quick and controlled.

Strength + speed circuit (3 rounds, 40 sec on / 20 sec off), all in place: squats → push-ups (knees OK) → mountain climbers → plank hold → in-place fast feet. Rest a minute between rounds.

Finish: The normal stretch — calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, groin, ~20 seconds each — then trade one "proud moment" and a high-five.

🎉 Make it fun: Reaction round — Dad claps and you switch instantly (one clap = fast feet on the ball, two claps = freeze in a plank). Miss a call and you owe five squats. 😄
