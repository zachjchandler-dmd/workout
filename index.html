Warm-up · 5 min

Cone Steal Reaction Dash

Setup: Put 3 cones in a little cluster in the middle. You and son stand facing each other about 5 yards back on each side, ready position — knees soft, on your toes. No ball on run days, so this is all quick feet and reaction.

How it works: Dad calls out signals — "GO!" means both of you dash in and try to grab a cone before the other; the one who grabs first wins the point. Mix in fake-outs like "Green… GO!" and fun fakes ("Grrr… GRAPES!") that are not the go word. Only move on the real "GO!". First to 5 cones wins, then swap who's the caller.

Cues: Stay low and loaded, weight on the balls of your feet, explode on the first step, and reset fast after each round.

🎉 Make it fun: Sneak in silly fake words that almost sound like "go" — "gooooal!", "goat!", "guacamole!" — and whoever jumps early does a goofy chicken dance. The suspense is half the fun.

Both start 5 yards back and explode to the middle on the go signal — grab a cone to score.

Warm-up · 5 min

Dynamic Running Warm-Up Flow

Setup: Mark a lane about 20 yards long with a cone at each end. You'll travel down the lane doing a movement, then jog easy back to the start and pick the next one.

How it works: Move down the lane with each of these, one per length, jogging back between: march with high knees · butt-kicks · walking lunges · A-skips (skip and drive the knee up) · side shuffles (face the same way down and back) · straight-leg "toy soldier" kicks · finish with 2 smooth build-up strides (start easy, glide to about 70% by the far cone). Do them together and copy each other's form.

Cues: Tall posture, arms bent and pumping, land soft and quiet, and stay relaxed — this wakes the legs up, it's not a race.

🎉 Make it fun: Call each movement a "level" like a video game — clear all 7 levels to "unlock" the run. Son gets to invent one bonus silly move for Dad to copy at the end.

One drill down the 20-yard lane, easy jog back, then the next — seven movements to warm the legs.

Endurance · ~16 min

Landmark Adventure Run

Setup: Head out on the sidewalk, a quiet street, or a loop around the yard/park. No measuring needed — you'll run by landmarks and by feel. This is his first logged run, so we start gentle: about 12 minutes of actual jogging, broken into friendly chunks.

How it works: Do 4 rounds of 3 minutes easy jog + 1 minute walk (~16 min total). To make the jog fly by, before each one let son pick a "landmark" to jog toward — a mailbox, a big tree, the stop sign. Jog to it (and keep an easy pace past it until the 3 minutes are up), then walk the recovery minute and pick the next landmark together. Keep it to a conversational pace — if he can't chat, ease off.

Cues: Relax the shoulders, easy breathing, short light steps. Slow and steady wins — the only goal is to keep moving and finish smiling.

🎉 Make it fun: Turn it into a story — each landmark is a "checkpoint" on a treasure map, and the walk minute is where you "read the next clue." Name the route ("The Dragon Loop") and it becomes his run.

Jog to a chosen landmark for 3 minutes, walk a minute to recover, repeat four times — about 12 minutes of running.

Speed & Fun · ~10 min

Countdown Rocket Sprints & Tail Tag

Setup: Lay a speed stick (or cone) as a start line, then put cones at 10, 15, and 20 yards out. For Tail Tag, each of you tucks a rag or flag belt loosely into the back of your waistband.

How it works: Part 1 — Rocket Sprints: Dad does a countdown "3… 2… 1… BLAST OFF!" and son sprints all-out to the 10-yard cone, walks back. Then the 15, then the 20. Do the ladder twice. Add a couple of handicap races — son starts a few yards ahead and tries to reach the cone before Dad. Part 2 — Tail Tag: in a small area, chase and try to snatch each other's tail; whoever grabs the other's tail first wins the round. Best of 5.

Cues: Drive the arms, push hard on the first 3 steps, and lean slightly forward out of the start. In Tail Tag, stay light and change direction quick.

🎉 Make it fun: Let son invent the launch call ("Banana… SPLIT!") and give Dad a silly handicap — Dad has to start sitting down, or spin once before chasing. Level the playing field and the giggles go up.

Explode off the start line on the countdown to each cone — 10, then 15, then 20 yards — walking back between.

Cooldown · ~8 min

Walk, Stretch & Chat

Walk it off: An easy 3–4 minute walk together until your breathing settles all the way down — no rush.

Stretch: Calves, quads, hamstrings, and hips — hold each about 20 seconds, nice and gentle. Legs did the work today, so give them a little love.

Chat & recap: While you stretch, each of you names one thing the other did great — a strong sprint, a landmark he powered to, a good attitude on a tired jog. Finish with a high-five and a big sip of water.

🌧️ Rain Backup (small-space, ~35 min)

Storm rolls in or it's just too hot? No running room needed — a couple of square feet each is plenty. We keep the legs and lungs working indoors.

Cardio circuit (3 rounds of 40 sec on / 20 sec rest): march-in-place high knees · butt-kicks in place · fast feet (tiny quick steps on the spot) · star jumps · standing mountain climbers. All in one spot — no space needed.

Strength circuit (2 rounds): 30 seconds each — squats · push-ups (knees OK) · plank hold · glute bridges · calf raises. Rest a minute between rounds.

Finish: The normal stretch — calves, quads, hamstrings, hips, ~20 seconds each — then trade one "great thing" and a high-five.

🎉 Make it fun: High-knee showdown — both count knee-drives for 30 seconds and try to beat your own number. Winner picks the next move in the circuit.
