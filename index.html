Warm-up · 5 min

Cone Color Dribble Dash

Setup: Scatter 5–6 cones (different colors if you've got them, otherwise number them out loud) around a 12×12 yard area. Son starts in the middle with a ball at his feet.

How it works: Dad calls out a color (or number) and Son dribbles the ball to that cone, taps around it, and dribbles back to the middle — then Dad calls the next one. Start slow and easy, then speed up the calls as you warm up. Every minute, swap so Dad dribbles and Son is the caller.

Cues: Head up so you can hear and spot the cone, little push touches to steer, and keep the ball within a step of your feet.

🎉 Make it fun: Throw in a fake-out call — shout a color, then quickly change it before he gets there. Add a "double" call (two cones in a row) worth a bonus point for a clean run.

Son dribbles to whatever color Dad shouts and back to the middle — quick reactions, ball always under control.

Warm-up · 5 min

Jungle Animal Moves

Setup: Set two cones about 10 yards apart to mark a start and finish line. No ball for this one — just your bodies and some laughs.

How it works: Travel from one cone to the other doing an animal move, then jog back and pick a new one: bear crawl (hands & feet, hips low), crab walk (belly up), frog jumps, bunny hops, giant lunge "dinosaur" steps, and finish with fast "cheetah" skips. Take turns being the one who picks the next animal.

Cues: Big controlled movements to wake the legs and hips up — this loosens everything before the sharp stuff. Breathe and go at a comfy pace.

🎉 Make it fun: Everyone has to make the animal's sound while they move — loudest roar wins. Whoever picks "cheetah" gets a real little race to the finish line.

Bear crawl, crab walk, frog jump and skip between the cones — a no-ball wake-up for the legs and hips.

Technical · ~8 min

Turn Master Gates

Setup: Put two cones about 8 yards apart as a "there-and-back" lane. You'll dribble to one cone, turn, dribble back, turn, repeat. Pick one turn to practice at a time.

How it works: Dribble up to the cone and make a clean turn, then push out the other way. Cycle through three turns: pull-back (sole of the foot drags the ball back), inside hook (turn it with the inside of your foot), and the Cruyff (fake a pass, then drag the ball behind your standing leg). Once you've got the feel, count how many clean turns you can string together in 60 seconds without losing the ball.

Cues: Get close to the cone before you turn, use the sole or inside of the foot to grab the ball, and accelerate out of the turn — the turn is only good if you burst away after it.

🎉 Make it fun: Dad shouts the turn name at the last second so Son has to react — call "Cruyff!" or "pull-back!" and see if he nails the right one. Cleanest turn of the day gets named "move of the morning."

Dribble the 8-yard lane, turn cleanly at each cone, and accelerate out — count how many clean turns in a minute.

Technical · ~7 min

Weak-Foot Wall Rally

Setup: Stand about 4–5 yards from the rebounder wall (or any flat wall). Today's rule: weak foot only — the one you don't usually use.

How it works: Pass the ball into the wall with your weak foot, take a soft first touch to control the rebound, and pass again — all weak foot. Count how many clean weak-foot passes in a row you can keep going without the ball getting away. It'll feel wobbly at first — that's exactly the point. A strong weak foot is a superpower on the field.

Cues: Lock your ankle and strike through the middle of the ball, plant your standing foot beside it, and take a calm cushioned first touch before the next pass.

🎉 Make it fun: Play "weak-foot HORSE" — every clean pass adds a letter; first to spell a silly word (S-O-C-C-E-R) wins. Miss and you keep your letters but the streak count resets to zero.

Pass and control off the wall using only the weak foot — count the clean weak-foot passes you string together.

Speed & Agility · ~15 min

Comet Shuttle

Setup: Build a three-part line: (1) 4–5 speed sticks flat on the ground about 1.5 feet apart, (2) a turn cone about 8 yards past the sticks, and (3) 2–3 trapezoid hurdles a couple feet apart on the way back. No ball for this block.

How it works: Go on Dad's "GO": lateral quick feet sideways through the speed sticks, sprint out to the turn cone, plant and cut, then two-foot hops back over the trapezoid hurdles and sprint through the start line to stop the clock. Walk back to recover, then try to beat your time. Do 4–6 runs total.

Cues: Fast light feet through the sticks (don't touch them), pump your arms on the sprint, sink your hips to plant-and-cut at the cone, and land soft on the hops.

🎉 Make it fun: Name it "The Comet" and give each run a countdown launch — "3… 2… 1… blast off!" Handicap race: Dad runs it too but starts a beat later so it's a real photo finish.

Lateral quick feet through the sticks, sprint to the turn cone, then hop the hurdles home — fastest run wins.

Game · ~15 min

Beat the Keeper Shootout

Setup: Make one goal about 5–6 feet wide with cones. Put a start cone about 12 yards out, with a "defender gate" (two cones a few feet apart) about halfway that the shooter has to dribble through first. Dad is the keeper.

How it works: Son dribbles from the start, pushes the ball through the defender gate, and takes a shot to beat the keeper — Dad can come off his line to make it real. Take 10 shots and count how many go in, then swap so Son goes in goal and Dad shoots. Play best-of for bragging rights and keep score out loud.

Cues: Look up to spot where the keeper isn't, pick your corner early, and pass the ball into the net (placement) rather than blasting it. A little change of pace through the gate throws the keeper off.

🎉 Make it fun: Call every shot like a penalty in the World Cup final — "this is to WIN it!" Bonus point for a shot into the far corner, and a full slide celebration for every goal.

Dribble through the gate and finish past Dad in goal — 10 shots, count the goals, then trade places.

Cooldown · ~5 min

Walk, Stretch & Recap

Walk it off: An easy lap or two together until your breathing settles — no rush, sip some water.

Stretch: Calves, quads, hamstrings, hips, and a gentle shoulder roll — hold each about 20 seconds. Lots of turns and sprints today, so give those legs some love.

Recap one great thing: Each of you names one awesome thing the other did — a slick Cruyff turn, a rocket weak-foot pass, a corner finish past the keeper. Finish with a high-five and a big drink of water.

🌧️ Rain Backup (small-space, ~45 min)

Storm rolls in or the heat's just too much? No running room needed — a couple of square feet each is plenty. We keep it fun with the ball at your feet, weak-foot work, and a bodyweight circuit.

Ball mastery ladder (2 rounds, 40 sec each, tiny space): toe taps · sole rolls side-to-side · foundations (inside-inside) · sole-roll pull-backs · around-the-ball taps. Count your toe taps in the last 30 seconds and try to beat it.

Weak-foot in place: Standing sole rolls and short inside-foot taps with the weak foot only for 40 seconds — same superpower we're building outside, just in place.

Strength & hops circuit (2 rounds): 30 seconds each — squats · push-ups (knees OK) · plank hold · standing mountain climbers · little two-foot jumps. Rest a minute between rounds.

Finish: The normal stretch — calves, quads, hamstrings, hips, ~20 seconds each — then trade one "great thing" and a high-five.

🎉 Make it fun: Plank staring contest — hold the plank and try to make each other laugh. First to smile does five extra toe taps. 😄
