Warm-up · 5 min

Copycat Locomotion

Setup: A loose open area about 15 yards across, with a couple of cones just to mark the space. No ball — this one's all footwork.

How it works: One of you is the leader and jogs around the area, mixing in moves — side-shuffles, backpedals, skips, quick stops, high-knee bursts, a spin. The follower stays about 3 yards behind and copies everything in real time. Go 60 seconds, then switch leader. Do a couple of rounds each.

Cues: Stay light on your feet, react fast, keep your eyes on the leader, and gradually wake up every gear.

🎉 Make it fun: Leader gets to throw in one goofy move each round — a robot walk, a dinosaur stomp, a slow-motion sprint — and the follower has to nail it.

Leader invents the path and the moves; follower mirrors every one.

Warm-up · 5 min

Running Form ABCs

Setup: A lane about 15 yards long — mark the start and finish with a cone, and lay the speed sticks flat down the middle as a visual guide. Line up side by side.

How it works: Move down the lane doing one form drill at a time, then walk back easy. Go through: marching high knees → A-skips → butt-kicks → karaoke (grapevine) side-steps → straight-leg bounds (Frankenstein kicks). One length each, nice and controlled.

Cues: Tall posture, arms driving front-to-back (not across), land softly on the balls of your feet, quick light contacts.

🎉 Make it fun: Call each drill a "level" — clear all five levels without giggling too hard and you both "level up" to the run.

Work down the lane one form drill at a time; stroll back to reset.

Endurance · ~18 min

Run-Walk Intervals — Your First Morning Run

Setup: Any safe stretch you can jog — a loop around the yard, a quiet sidewalk, or a park path. Nothing to carry but water and a timer.

How it works: This is the classic beginner run-walk. Do 6 rounds of 2 minutes easy jog, then 1 minute walk. That's about 12 minutes of actual running your very first time out — perfect. Keep the jog at a "we can still chat" pace. If 6 rounds feels like plenty at 5, that's totally fine — this is round one and we build from here.

Cues: Conversational pace — if you can't talk, ease off. Short relaxed strides, loose shoulders, breathe with a rhythm. The walk breaks are part of the plan, not a cheat.

🎉 Make it fun: Pick a "chat topic" for each jog interval — best pizza topping, dream superpower, funniest thing at school — and keep the conversation going the whole 2 minutes. If you can chatter, your pace is right.

Jog the top of the loop, walk the bottom — repeat six times. Chat the whole way.

Speed & Fun · ~9 min

Rocket Relays & Handicap Races

Setup: Two cones about 20 yards apart on flat ground. Clear the path both ways.

How it works: Part A — Shuttle relay (4 min): sprint to the far cone, touch it, sprint back, then tag your partner to go. Add up your team touches for 3 rounds. Part B — Handicap races (5 min): son gets a fair head start and Dad has to chase him down over 20 yards — adjust the head start each race so they finish close. Time a couple of son's dashes with the phone if you want a number for the record book.

Cues: Explode from the start, drive your arms, run tall through the finish — don't slow down until you're past the cone.

🎉 Make it fun: Winner of each race calls the "start signal" for the next one — "Ready, set, BANANA!" — and you can only go on the real word. Fake-outs = giggles.

Son starts ahead; Dad chases. Even up the head start so it's a photo finish.

Cooldown · ~8 min

Walk, Stretch & Chat

Walk it off: Easy walk together for 3 minutes until your breathing settles all the way down.

Stretch: Calves, quads, hamstrings, and hip flexors — hold each about 20 seconds, relaxed and gentle. Legs did the work today, so give them a little love.

Chat: Each of you shares a "high" from the run and one thing you're proud the other person did. Finish with a high-five and a big "we're runners now" cheer.

🌧️ Rain Backup (small-space, ~30–35 min)

Storming or soaked? No running room needed — just a couple of square feet each. This keeps the heart pumping indoors on a running day.

Cardio circuit (3 rounds, side by side): 40 seconds of work, 20 seconds rest, straight through — marching high knees in place · squats · fast feet (tiny quick steps on the spot) · push-ups (knees OK) · mountain climbers · plank hold · jumping jacks. Rest a minute between rounds. Low ceiling? Swap jumping jacks for stepping up and down a single stair.

In-place "race" game (3 rounds): On "go," both do fast feet on the spot for 20 seconds and count your steps — try to beat your own number each round. Dad can spot son a head-start handicap on the count to keep it close.

Finish: The normal stretch — calves, quads, hamstrings, hip flexors, ~20 seconds each — then trade one "high" from the session and a high-five.

🎉 Make it fun: "Coach's call" — take turns shouting the next move like a goofy drill sergeant. Whoever counts the most fast-feet steps picks the victory dance.
