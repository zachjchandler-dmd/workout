Warm-up · 5 min

Tail Tag Dribble

Setup: Mark a 12×12-yard box with four cones. Each of you tucks a "tail" — a sock or pinnie — into the back of your waistband so it hangs out, and each has a ball at your feet. Ball on today (it's a soccer morning), so this doubles as a warm-up and a first touch of the day.

How it works: On "go," dribble around the box and try to snatch the other player's tail while keeping control of your own ball — and keeping your own tail safe. Grab a tail and you score a point; lost your ball out of the box, it doesn't count. Play 60–90 second rounds, reset tails, and go again. Keep it light and giggly.

Cues: Small touches, head up so you can hunt and protect at the same time, and shield your ball with your body when someone comes hunting. Use the whole box — don't get trapped in a corner.

🎉 Make it fun: Best of 5 tails wins the round and gets a superhero pose. Add a "freeze" rule — Dad shouts "FREEZE!" and whoever twitches or loses their ball gives up a tail. Try one round left-foot-only.

Dribble-and-hunt tag inside a box — steal the other player's tail while shielding your own ball. Warms up first touch, close control, and quick reactions all at once.

Warm-up · 5 min

Simon's Stretch & Move

Setup: Mark a short lane about 15 yards long with two cones. No ball for this one — it's a moving stretch to loosen hips, legs, and shoulders after the tag game. You'll travel down the lane doing one move, then jog back easy and start the next.

How it works: Dad (then Son) calls them out Simon-Says style, ~15 yards each: walking knee-hugs, walking lunges with a twist, toy-soldier straight-leg kicks, inchworms (walk hands out to a plank, walk feet up), side gallops both directions, and finish with high skips for height. Only move on "Simon says."

Cues: Slow and controlled — feel the stretch, don't yank. Tall posture, eyes up, breathe easy. This is the "wake the body up gently" part before the sharp stuff.

🎉 Make it fun: Real Simon Says rules — if Dad says "lunges!" without "Simon says" and you do it, you owe one silly dance move. Swap the caller each lap so you both get to trick each other.

A moving stretch down a short lane — knee-hugs, lunges with a twist, toy soldiers, inchworms, gallops, high skips — called Simon-Says style. Opens up the hips and legs for the sharp footwork ahead.

Technical · ~7 min

Scissor Gate Dribble

Setup: Build a line of 4–5 little "gates" (two cones a couple feet apart) about 4 yards apart, straight down the field. Each player has a ball. This is a moves-and-control drill — the goal is a clean scissors (step-over) at each gate.

How it works: Dribble toward the first gate, do one scissors just before it — swing your foot around the front of the ball as if you're going one way — then push the ball through the gate the other way with the outside of your foot and accelerate to the next gate. Repeat through every gate, jog back, go again. Take turns and count clean ones.

Cues: Sell the fake with your shoulders and eyes, keep the ball close, and explode after the move — the burst is what beats a defender. Start slow to get it clean, then speed up.

🎉 Make it fun: Name each gate after a defender to "beat." Every gate you get through with a clean scissors = 1 point; a double-scissors gate = 2. See how many clean moves you can stack in 60 seconds and log it below!

Dribble through a line of cone gates doing a scissors (step-over) at each and accelerating through. Builds a real move plus close control and the explosive touch that beats a defender.

Technical · ~8 min

Rebounder Two-Touch Passing

Setup: Stand about 4–5 yards from the rebounder wall with a ball. One player works while the other counts and cheers; then swap. This is first-touch-then-pass — the rhythm real passing is built on.

How it works: Pass the ball firmly into the wall. As it comes back, take a controlled first touch to set it slightly in front of you, then pass it right back with your other foot — touch, pass, touch, pass. That's a "two-touch" rep. Keep a smooth rhythm and count your clean reps in 60 seconds. Do a set with each foot leading.

Cues: Cushion the first touch — relax your foot so the ball dies close, don't stab at it. Lock your ankle and strike the middle of the ball with the inside of your foot on the pass. Stay on your toes, ready to move to the ball.

🎉 Make it fun: Call it "keepie catch" — every clean two-touch rep is a point, and a miss resets the count to zero, so how high can you climb? Try a round where you must say a fruit out loud on every pass.

Pass into the wall, cushion the rebound with a controlled first touch, then pass straight back — counting clean two-touch reps. The core rhythm of receiving and passing under a little pressure.

Speed & Agility · ~15 min

Lightning Bolt Agility Circuit

Setup: Build a three-station circuit, no ball (this is pure quickness): (1) lay the speed sticks flat in a ladder, (2) set 3–4 trapezoid hurdles a couple feet apart, (3) place 4 cones in a short zig-zag, then a final cone 10 yards out as the finish line.

How it works: Run it as one flowing circuit and time it: ladder — quick "one-foot-each-square" fast feet down the sticks; hurdles — two-footed hops over each; cones — sharp cut-and-weave through the zig-zag; then explode 10 yards through the finish. Walk back to recover and run it 4–6 times. Log your fastest clean run below.

Cues: Fast feet, not big feet — light and snappy on the ladder. Bend your knees and land soft on the hops. Get low and plant the outside foot to change direction, then sprint through the finish cone.

🎉 Make it fun: Dad runs the clock and does a dramatic race-announcer countdown. Beat your own best time and you earn a "Lightning Bolt" pose at the finish. Handicap it so Dad and Son finish neck-and-neck.

A timed quickness circuit — ladder fast-feet, two-footed hurdle hops, a cone-weave change of direction, and a 10-yard sprint finish. Speed, agility, and body control all in one flowing run.

Game · ~15 min

1v1 World Cup Showdown

Setup: Make two small goals with cones (about 3 feet wide) roughly 12–15 yards apart, facing each other. One ball. You're both attacker and defender — it's a pure 1v1 battle, best-of-your-choosing.

How it works: Whoever has the ball attacks their target goal; the other defends and tries to win it, then counter-attacks the other goal. Score a goal, restart from your own goal line. First to 5 goals wins the "match," then play a rematch. Keep the score out loud — winner of each match earns a country to play as next round.

Cues: Attacking — use the moves you practiced (scissors!), change speed, and go at the defender, don't run away from them. Defending — stay low, patient, and don't dive in; jockey until you can nick the ball. Celebrate the good goals, both of you.

🎉 Make it fun: Full World Cup energy — pick countries, do a goal celebration you've planned, and Dad does over-the-top commentary. Give Son a "golden goal" worth 2 if he beats Dad with a scissors move.

A 1v1 to two small goals — attack, defend, counter, first to five. The most fun, most competitive way to use every skill from the morning in a real battle.

Cooldown · 5 min

Walk, Stretch & Recap

Walk it off: Easy lap or two walking together until your breathing settles and you've cooled down from the 1v1 battles. Keep sipping water — it's a hot one coming.

Stretch: Give the legs some love — calves, quads, hamstrings, hips, and a gentle groin/butterfly stretch (lots of cutting and kicking today). Hold each about 20 seconds, relaxed, no bouncing.

Recap one great thing: While you stretch, each name your favorite moment — a clean scissors, a two-touch streak, a photo-finish circuit, a World Cup goal. Big high-five, then tap a button below so it counts. And if a drill was a blast, tap its ⭐! 🎉

🌧️ Rain Backup (small-space, ~45 min)

Storm rolls in early or the heat's too much? No field or running room needed — a couple of square feet each is plenty. We keep the ball skills that fit a tight space and add a bodyweight circuit for the energy.

Ball mastery in place (warm-up, 2 rounds of 90 sec): With the ball right at your feet — toe taps, then sole rolls side to side, then inside-outside taps. Quick light touches, head up. Rest 30 seconds between.

Foot-skill intervals (5 rounds, 40 sec on / 30 sec off): Rotate each round — fast toe taps · sole roll + tap · foundation (inside-inside) taps · pull-back V's · scissors over the ball. All on the spot, ball never leaving your little square.

Strength circuit (2 rounds): 30 seconds each — squats · push-ups (knees OK) · plank hold · mountain climbers · glute bridges. Rest a minute between rounds.

Finish: The normal stretch — calves, quads, hamstrings, hips, ~20 seconds each — then trade one "great thing" and a high-five.

🎉 Make it fun: Reaction round — Dad claps and you switch foot-skill instantly (one clap = toe taps, two claps = freeze with your foot on the ball). Miss a call and you owe five squats. 😄
